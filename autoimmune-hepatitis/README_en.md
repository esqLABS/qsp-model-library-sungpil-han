## Overview
Autoimmune hepatitis (AIH) is a chronic autoimmune liver disease in which
autoreactive T cells attack hepatocytes, accompanied by autoantibodies
(ANA, ASMA, LKM-1) and hypergammaglobulinaemia. Global prevalence is
roughly 10-17 per 100,000, more common in women, and progresses to
cirrhosis and liver failure if untreated. The core pathogenic mechanism is
direct hepatocyte damage by autoreactive Th1/Th17 cells together with
impaired regulatory T-cell (Treg) function. First-line treatment is
prednisolone alone or combined with azathioprine; MMF or rituximab is used
for refractory patients.

## Key Pathophysiological Pathways
| Pathway | Key molecules/mechanism | Clinical outcome |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| Autoreactive T-cell activation | HLA-DR3/DR4, molecular mimicry, CTLA-4 polymorphism | Direct cytotoxicity to hepatocytes |
| Th1-driven inflammation | IFN-γ, TNF-α overproduction | Hepatocyte necrosis, ALT spikes |
| Treg dysfunction | Reduced FoxP3 expression, TGF-β deficiency | Loss of self-tolerance, chronic activation |
| B cells/autoantibodies | ANA/ASMA/LKM-1, increased IgG | Further hepatocyte damage, diagnostic markers |
| Fibrotic progression | TGF-β, stellate cell activation | Increased risk of cirrhosis |
| GR-mediated immune suppression | Glucocorticoid receptor, NF-κB inhibition | Steroid-induced remission |

## Key Drug Targets
- **Prednisolone**: glucocorticoid receptor -> inhibits NF-κB/AP-1, promotes Treg — first-line induction therapy
- **Azathioprine -> 6-TGN**: HGPRT pathway -> inhibits DNA synthesis, reduces lymphocyte proliferation — standard maintenance therapy
- **Budesonide**: extensive first-pass metabolism -> liver-selective action, reduced systemic side effects — for non-cirrhotic AIH
- **MMF/MPA**: IMPDH inhibition -> blocks T-/B-cell proliferation — second-line therapy for azathioprine intolerance
- **Rituximab (anti-CD20)**: B-cell depletion -> reduces autoantibodies — for refractory/relapsing AIH

## mrgsolve Model (ODE Model)
- **Compartment structure**: drug PK compartments (prednisolone
  2-compartment, azathioprine -> 6-MP -> 6-TGN, MMF -> MPA, rituximab
  2-compartment + TMDD CD20), immune compartments (GR occupancy, Th1,
  Treg, B cells, autoantibodies), cytokines (IFN-γ, TGF-β, IL-6), liver
  injury index, serum ALT
- **Key treatment scenarios**: ① no treatment (natural history), ②
  prednisolone alone, ③ prednisolone + azathioprine (standard), ④
  budesonide + azathioprine, ⑤ prednisolone + MMF, ⑥ rituximab +
  prednisolone (refractory disease)
- **Calibration/basis**: reference to IAIHG (International Autoimmune
  Hepatitis Group) diagnostic criteria and ALT/IgG time-series data from
  major clinical trials (Czaja, Manns, Zachou, and others)

## Shiny Dashboard
Consists of tabs for patient profile (subtype, severity, weight),
pharmacokinetics (plasma concentration of each drug), liver function
indices (changes in ALT, IgG, bilirubin), immune biomarkers (Th1/Treg
ratio, autoantibody titre), scenario comparison (6-way overlay), and
clinical endpoints (complete biochemical remission rate, relapse
prediction).
