## Summary of the main clinical trials

| Trial | Drug | N | Duration | Main result |
|---------|------|---|------|----------|
| FABRY-001 (Eng 2001 NEJM) | agalsidase beta 1 mg/kg Q2W | 58 | 20 weeks | clearance of Gb3 from kidney, heart, and skin |
| Banikazemi 2007 AIM | agalsidase beta 1 mg/kg Q2W | 82 | 35 months | a 61% reduction in the composite renal, cardiac, and cerebral event |
| Schiffmann 2001 AIM | agalsidase alfa 0.2 mg/kg Q2W | 26 | 6 months | neuropathic pain improved, lyso-Gb3 fell |
| ATTRACT (Germain 2016 NEJM) | migalastat 150 mg QOD | 50 | 18 months | non-inferior to ERT, amenable mutations only |
| Hughes 2017 Lancet | migalastat 150 mg QOD | 36 | 18 months | eGFR -0.3 against -1.0 mL/min/yr on ERT |
| BRIGHT (Schiffmann 2021 JAMA) | pegunigalsidase alfa 1 mg/kg Q4W | 78 | 12 months | eGFR stable, lyso-Gb3 -50% |
| MODIFY (Lenders 2022 Lancet DE) | lucerastat 1000 mg TID | 118 | 12 months | BPI-SF -1.5 points, Gb3 fell |
