## Overview

Addison's disease (primary adrenal insufficiency) is a disease in which autoimmune destruction of the adrenal cortex chronically depletes cortisol and aldosterone secretion, with a prevalence in the West of about 100–140 per 100,000 population. In the autoimmune mechanism, T cell-mediated destruction of adrenocortical cells and autoantibodies against 21-hydroxylase (CYP21A2) are central. Cortisol deficiency abolishes negative feedback on the HPA axis, markedly raising ACTH/CRH, and aldosterone deficiency causes hyponatraemia, hyperkalaemia, and hypotension. Adrenal crisis is a life-threatening emergency, requiring increased steroid dosing under conditions of stress. The PK/PD of hydrocortisone and fludrocortisone replacement therapy is central to the model.

## Key Pathways

| Pathway | Key Molecule/Mechanism | Clinical Outcome |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| HPA axis dysregulation | CRH → elevated ACTH (loss of negative feedback) | Skin hyperpigmentation (excess ACTH-MSH) |
| Cortisol deficiency | Decreased GR occupancy → release of immune suppression | Fatigue, hypotension, hypoglycaemia |
| Aldosterone deficiency | Decreased MR occupancy → decreased renal Na⁺ reabsorption | Hyponatraemia, hyperkalaemia, dehydration |
| Autoimmune destruction | CD4⁺/CD8⁺ T cells, 21-OHase antibodies | Adrenocortical fibrosis/loss |
| Deficient stress response | Inability to surge cortisol → adrenal crisis | Shock, risk of death |
| Cortisol circadian rhythm | Loss of HPA axis circadian rhythm | Sleep disturbance, metabolic abnormality |
| DHEA deficiency | Deficient adrenal androgens | Fatigue, decreased sexual function (especially in women) |

## Drug Targets

- **Hydrocortisone**: cortisol replacement; 15–25 mg/day in divided doses, aiming to mimic the circadian rhythm
- **Fludrocortisone**: mineralocorticoid replacement; regulates blood pressure/electrolytes
- **DHEA (dehydroepiandrosterone)**: supplementation for quality-of-life improvement in female patients
- **Stress dosing (sick day rules)**: protocol to increase dose 2–3-fold during fever/surgery
- **Continuous hydrocortisone infusion pump**: under investigation to reproduce the physiological circadian pattern

## mrgsolve Model (ODE Model)

- **Compartment structure**: hydrocortisone (oral, 2-compartment PK) + fludrocortisone (2 compartments) + DHEA compartment + PD compartments for CRH · ACTH · cortisol (endogenous) · GR-free · GR-bound · GR-mRNA · sodium · potassium · blood pressure · blood glucose · BMD · ACTH-MSH signalling · residual adrenal tissue · adrenal crisis risk
- **Key treatment scenarios**: ① untreated (pre-diagnosis), ② standard HC in 3 divided doses, ③ morning-weighted HC, ④ circadian pump infusion, ⑤ stress-dose response, ⑥ HC + fludrocortisone + DHEA triple replacement
- **Calibration/rationale**: parameters referenced from the Bornstein et al. Endocrine Society guidelines and Johannsson et al. circadian HC clinical trial

## Shiny Dashboard

Consists of a patient profile tab (weight, sex, residual adrenal function), an HC/fludrocortisone PK and cortisol circadian profile tab, an electrolyte/blood pressure dynamics tab, an HPA axis biomarker tab (ACTH/CRH), a treatment scenario comparison tab (standard vs. circadian vs. stress dosing), and a long-term outcomes tab (BMD/metabolism).
