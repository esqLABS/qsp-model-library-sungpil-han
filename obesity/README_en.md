## Overview

Obesity is a chronic metabolic disease defined as BMI ≥ 30 kg/m², with a global adult prevalence of roughly 16% (about 800 million people) and still rising. The core pathogenesis is dysregulation of energy-intake and energy-expenditure homeostasis, involving the interaction of leptin resistance in the hypothalamic melanocortin-4 receptor (MC4R) pathway, reduced GLP-1/GIP secretion, accelerated gastric emptying, and insulin resistance. GLP-1 and dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonists (semaglutide, tirzepatide) are changing the treatment paradigm, achieving 10–22% weight loss in clinical use.

## Key Pathophysiological Pathways

| Pathway | Key molecules/mechanism | Clinical outcome |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| Leptin-melanocortin axis | Leptin resistance → reduced MC4R activity → failure of appetite suppression | Overeating, weight gain |
| Reduced GLP-1/GIP secretion | Impaired L-cell/K-cell function → diminished incretin effect | Insufficient insulin secretion, accelerated gastric emptying |
| Insulin resistance | Impaired IRS-1/PI3K/Akt signalling → reduced hepatic/muscle/adipose insulin response | Elevated fasting glucose, increased HOMA-IR |
| Chronic adipose tissue inflammation | M1 macrophage infiltration → TNF-α, IL-6 → adipocyte insulin resistance | Systemic chronic inflammation |
| Reduced energy expenditure | Decreased adaptive thermogenesis | Weight regain after weight loss |
| Gut microbiota dysbiosis | Increased Firmicutes/Bacteroidetes ratio → increased energy extraction | Worsened obesity, fatty liver |
| Elevated ghrelin/NPY/AgRP | Elevated orexigenic peptides | Increased appetite, sustained weight gain |

## Key Drug Targets

- **Semaglutide (GLP-1RA)**: activates the GLP-1 receptor → suppresses appetite, delays gastric emptying, promotes insulin secretion (STEP-1: ~15% weight loss)
- **Tirzepatide (dual GLP-1/GIPR agonist)**: simultaneous activation of GLP-1 and GIP receptors → ~21% weight loss (SURMOUNT-1)
- **Orlistat (lipase inhibitor)**: blocks ~30% of gastrointestinal fat absorption
- **Phentermine/topiramate**: central sympathetic activation + GABA receptor modulation → appetite suppression
- **Naltrexone/bupropion**: enhances the melanocortin pathway, suppresses the reward pathway

## mrgsolve Model (ODE Model)

- **Compartment structure**: 2 SC compartments for semaglutide, 2 SC compartments for tirzepatide, 2 oral compartments for orlistat, 1 CNS (hypothalamus) compartment, GLP1R/GIPR occupancy compartment; food intake, gastric emptying, ghrelin, insulin, blood glucose, body fat, body weight, leptin, triglycerides, HbA1c, systemic inflammation, HOMA-IR
- **Key treatment scenarios**: ① placebo ② semaglutide 2.4 mg SC QW ③ tirzepatide 15 mg SC QW ④ orlistat 120 mg TID ⑤ phentermine/topiramate oral QD
- **Calibration/evidence base**: referenced against weight-loss curves from the STEP-1 (semaglutide), SURMOUNT-1 (tirzepatide), and XENDOS (orlistat) clinical trials

## Shiny Dashboard

Consists of 6 tabs: ① **Patient profile** (sets baseline BMI, weight, diabetes status, leptin resistance), ② **PK** (plasma drug concentrations and GLP-1R/GIPR receptor occupancy), ③ **Key PD markers** (trends in appetite, blood glucose, insulin, HOMA-IR), ④ **Clinical endpoints** (weight-loss percentage, BMI, waist-circumference change), ⑤ **Scenario comparison** (direct comparison of 5 treatment strategies), ⑥ **Biomarkers** (trends in leptin, triglycerides, HbA1c, systemic inflammation).
