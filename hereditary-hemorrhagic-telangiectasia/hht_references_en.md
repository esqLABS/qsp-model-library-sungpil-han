## 3. ★ The BMP9–ALK1–endoglin axis and the shear stress set point

**The basis for the central claim of this model.** ALK1/SMAD1/5 is not a simple brake on angiogenesis but
**a mechanotransduction pathway activated by flow (shear stress)**, and in the normal state a rise in shear
creates the negative feedback that drives *inward* remodelling. Lose this pathway and
**the sign of the feedback flips** — the model's `REMOD = KSH·g(WSS)·(1 − 2·pSMAD)` term.

## 9. ★ Treatment — tranexamic acid and topical therapy (a structural ceiling)

## 11. ★ Iron metabolism · anaemia · transfusion (where the threshold arises)

The decisive result of the model comes from here: the iron content of 1 mL of blood is `0.0347 × Hb` (mg), and
because hepcidin caps daily absorption at about 10 mg, **the greatest blood loss oral iron can keep up
with is about 17.8 mL/day**. Whether a patient crosses that line decides the clinical course.
