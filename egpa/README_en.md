## Overview

Eosinophilic granulomatosis with polyangiitis (EGPA, formerly Churg-Strauss
syndrome) is a rare ANCA-associated vasculitis characterised by the triad of
asthma, eosinophilia, and small-vessel granulomatous inflammation, with an
annual incidence of roughly 0.5-3 per million. Its pathogenesis combines
IL-5-mediated eosinophil hyperproliferation and a Th2-skewed immune
response with, in some patients, ANCA (MPO)-positive small-vessel
vasculitis. Mild disease with no active organ damage (heart, nerves,
kidney) is treated with steroids alone, while moderate-to-severe or
steroid-dependent disease is treated with mepolizumab (anti-IL-5) as
standard of care.

## Key Pathophysiological Pathways

| Pathway | Key molecules/mechanism | Clinical outcome |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| IL-5 pathway | IL-5 -> eosinophil maturation in marrow, entry into blood, tissue infiltration | Marked increase in blood eosinophil count |
| Th2-skewed immunity | IL-4, IL-13 overexpression -> IgE generation, mucosal inflammation | Worsening asthma, nasal polyps |
| Eosinophil tissue damage | Release of ECP, MBP, EPO -> toxicity to vessels, myocardium, nerves | Myocarditis, peripheral neuropathy |
| ANCA (MPO) pathway | Anti-MPO ANCA -> neutrophil activation -> small-vessel inflammation | Glomerulonephritis, alveolar haemorrhage |
| Granuloma formation | Macrophages/giant cells -> perivascular granuloma | Organ dysfunction |
| Asthmatic airway inflammation | Eosinophils, mast cells, IgE -> bronchial hyperreactivity | Reduced FEV1, asthma attacks |
| Cardiac involvement | Eosinophilic myocarditis/endocarditis (Löffler) | Heart failure, arrhythmia risk |

## Key Drug Targets

- **Prednisolone (systemic corticosteroid)**: broad immunosuppression, induces eosinophil apoptosis
- **Mepolizumab**: anti-IL-5 monoclonal antibody -> reduces eosinophils, improved remission rate in the MIRRA trial
- **Benralizumab**: anti-IL-5Rα -> directly depletes eosinophils/basophils (via NK-cell ADCC mechanism)
- **Rituximab**: B-cell depletion in ANCA-positive severe EGPA
- **Azathioprine/methotrexate**: maintenance therapy for steroid tapering

## mrgsolve Model (ODE Model)

- **Compartment structure**: drug PK compartments (prednisolone/
  mepolizumab/benralizumab depot/central/peripheral) + PD compartments
  (free/bound IL-5, eosinophil precursors/blood/tissue, Th2 cytokines,
  ANCA, vascular damage index, asthma FEV1, cardiac involvement index,
  IgE, granuloma burden)
- **Key treatment scenarios**: ① untreated (natural history), ② prednisolone
  alone, ③ mepolizumab + prednisolone (based on the MIRRA trial), ④
  benralizumab + prednisolone, ⑤ rituximab + prednisolone (ANCA-positive
  severe disease), ⑥ steroid-tapering maintenance therapy (azathioprine)
- **Calibration/basis**: reference to the MIRRA trial (mepolizumab), the
  MANDARA trial (benralizumab), and EGPA cohort data such as Guillevin et al.

## Shiny Dashboard

Consists of 6 tabs: ① patient profile (set ANCA status, baseline
eosinophil count, FEV1, organ involvement), ② PK tab (blood concentrations
of biologics and steroids), ③ immune PD tab (trends in IL-5, eosinophils,
Th2 cytokines), ④ clinical endpoints (remission rate, number of relapses,
FEV1, cardiac indices), ⑤ scenario comparison (simultaneous comparison of
6 treatment strategies), ⑥ biomarkers (trends in IgE, ANCA titre, vascular
damage index).
