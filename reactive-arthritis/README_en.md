## Overview

Reactive arthritis (ReA) is a sterile arthritis that develops within a few weeks after a distant infection of the urethra, gut, or pharynx, and occurs frequently in HLA-B27-positive individuals. The main causative organisms are *Chlamydia trachomatis*, *Salmonella*, *Shigella*, *Yersinia*, *Campylobacter*, and others; antigenic components of these organisms persistently stimulate T cells within the joint, causing the triad (formerly Reiter syndrome) of asymmetric lower-limb arthritis, enthesitis, urethritis, and conjunctivitis. NSAIDs and antibiotics form the basis of treatment during the acute phase, while sulfasalazine, immunosuppressants, and biologics (anti-TNF) are used in chronic or relapsing cases.

## Key Pathways

| Pathway | Key Molecule/Mechanism | Clinical Outcome |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| HLA-B27-associated immunity | HLA-B27 heavy chain misfolding (UPR) → IL-23 overexpression | Th17 pathway activation, predisposition to enthesitis |
| Persistent infectious antigen | Bacterial LPS · peptides → TLR activation of joint macrophages | Sustained secretion of IL-1β · TNF · IL-6 |
| Th1/Th17 activation | CD4+ Th1 (IFN-γ) · Th17 (IL-17) → induction of synoviocytes and RANKL | Synovial inflammation, bone erosion |
| CD8+ T cell cytotoxicity | HLA-B27 → bacterial peptide presentation → CTL activation → joint tissue damage | Chronic synovial damage |
| RANKL/OPG imbalance | Inflammatory cytokines → elevated RANKL → osteoclast activation | Periarticular bone loss |
| Asymmetric enthesitis | IL-17 + mechanical stress → activation of enthesis fibroblasts | Calcaneal and knee enthesis pain |
| Antimicrobial immunity-autoimmunity crossover | Molecular mimicry → autoantigen response | Tendency toward relapse and chronicity |

## Drug Targets

- **NSAIDs (naproxen, indomethacin, diclofenac)**: COX inhibition → decreased PGE2; first-line treatment for acute joint pain and swelling
- **Antibiotics (doxycycline, azithromycin)**: clearance of persistent bacterial antigen; effective in *Chlamydia*-associated ReA
- **Corticosteroids**: intra-articular injection or systemic; short-term adjunct when NSAIDs are insufficient
- **Sulfasalazine**: DMARD for chronic peripheral joint involvement; moderate clinical evidence
- **Methotrexate/azathioprine**: immunosuppression for chronic relapsing cases
- **Anti-TNF agents (etanercept, adalimumab)**: off-label use in HLA-B27-positive axial involvement and refractory cases

## mrgsolve Model (ODE Model)

- **Compartment structure**: PK compartments for NSAIDs/antibiotics/sulfasalazine/anti-TNF + bacterial antigen load decay module, Th1 · Th17 activation dynamics, IL-17 · TNF · IL-1β production compartments, joint inflammation index (JII), including an enthesitis score compartment
- **Key treatment scenarios**: many, including untreated natural history, NSAID alone, doxycycline alone, NSAID+antibiotic, intra-articular steroid injection, sulfasalazine, methotrexate, etanercept, and etanercept+methotrexate combination
- **Calibration/rationale**: based on clinical data from Hannu et al. Best Pract Res Clin Rheumatol 2011, the Yli-Kerttula et al. *Ann Rheum Dis* antibiotic RCT, and the ASAS spondyloarthritis classification criteria

## Shiny Dashboard

Consists of 6 tabs: (1) **Patient Profile** — preceding infection type · HLA-B27 status · accompanying symptoms; (2) **PK Profile** — plasma concentrations of NSAIDs/antibiotics/biologics; (3) **Key PD Measures** — dynamics of bacterial antigen load · Th17 · IL-17 inhibition; (4) **Clinical Endpoints** — joint pain (VAS) · CRP · enthesitis score; (5) **Scenario Comparison** — comparison of inflammation resolution across treatment strategies; (6) **Biomarkers** — ESR, CRP, HLA-B27, synovial fluid cell count.
