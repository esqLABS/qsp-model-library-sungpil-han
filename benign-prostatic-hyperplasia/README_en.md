## Overview
Benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH) is the most common urological disease, experienced by about 50% of men aged 50 and over and more than 80% of those aged 80 and over. DHT (dihydrotestosterone) activates the androgen receptor (AR) to drive proliferation of prostatic stromal and epithelial cells, and α1-adrenergic receptor-mediated smooth muscle tone adds to this, producing lower urinary tract symptoms (LUTS). The core treatment targets are 5α-reductase (SRD5A1/2, blocking DHT production) and the α1-adrenergic receptor (smooth muscle relaxation), and PDE5 inhibitors show additional effects on the bladder neck and prostatic smooth muscle.

## Key Pathways
| Pathway | Key Molecule/Mechanism | Clinical Outcome |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| DHT-AR-mediated proliferation | Testosterone → 5α-reductase (SRD5A1/2) → DHT → AR activation | Increased prostate volume, elevated PSA |
| α1-AR-mediated tone | α1a/α1d receptors, smooth muscle contraction | Functional urethral obstruction, delayed voiding |
| Stromal-epithelial interaction | IGF-1, FGF, EGF, TGF-β signalling | Prostatic stromal hyperproliferation |
| Prostatic inflammation | COX-2, IL-8, NF-κB pathway | Acceleration of chronic inflammatory BPH |
| Bladder function changes | Detrusor hypertrophy, unstable contractions | Nocturia, frequency, urgency |
| cGMP-PDE5 pathway | NO → sGC → cGMP generation; PDE5 degradation | Bladder neck/urethral relaxation (tadalafil target) |

## Drug Targets
- **Tamsulosin (α1-blocker)**: selective antagonism of α1a/α1d receptors → relaxes prostatic/bladder neck smooth muscle — immediate LUTS improvement
- **Finasteride (SRD5A2 inhibitor)**: 40~70% reduction in DHT synthesis → prostate shrinkage — based on the PLESS trial
- **Dutasteride (dual SRD5A1/2 inhibitor)**: over 90% reduction in DHT → more complete AR blockade — based on the COMBAT trial
- **Tadalafil (PDE5 inhibitor)**: increases cGMP → smooth muscle relaxation — FDA-approved for BPH-LUTS (5 mg QD)
- **Dutasteride+tamsulosin combination**: simultaneous improvement of structure and function — COMBAT, CombAT trials

## mrgsolve Model (ODE Model)
- **Compartment structure**: drug PK (tamsulosin 3 compartments, finasteride 3 compartments, dutasteride 3 compartments, tadalafil 3 compartments), hormones (plasma testosterone/DHT, prostatic tissue DHT), the AR activation complex, structural/functional endpoints (prostate volume, cGMP, α1-AR occupancy, IPSS, maximum flow rate Qmax, post-void residual PVR, PSA, prostatic inflammation index)
- **Key treatment scenarios**: ① watchful waiting (untreated), ② tamsulosin 0.4 mg QD, ③ finasteride 5 mg QD, ④ dutasteride 0.5 mg QD, ⑤ dutasteride+tamsulosin combination, ⑥ tadalafil 5 mg QD
- **Calibration/rationale**: referenced against IPSS/Qmax/prostate volume change data from PLESS (finasteride, 4 years), CombAT (combination, 4 years), and the MTOPS trial

## Shiny Dashboard
Consists of tabs for patient profile (age, prostate volume, PSA, baseline IPSS), pharmacokinetics (plasma concentration-time for each drug), a hormone/AR tab (testosterone, DHT, AR occupancy), clinical endpoints (IPSS, Qmax, PVR, prostate volume), treatment scenario comparison (6-way overlay), and biomarkers (PSA, prostatic DHT, inflammation index).
