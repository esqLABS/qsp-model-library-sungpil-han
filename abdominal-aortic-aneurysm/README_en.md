## Overview

Abdominal aortic aneurysm (AAA) is an abnormal dilation of the abdominal aorta to a maximum diameter of 3 cm or more, with a prevalence of about 4–8% in men aged 70 and over. The core pathogenic mechanisms are loss of vascular smooth muscle cells (VSMC) in the aortic media, MMP (matrix metalloproteinase)-mediated degradation of extracellular matrix (ECM) components such as elastin and collagen, and chronic transmural inflammation driven by macrophages and T cells. Rupture risk increases markedly once aortic diameter exceeds 5.5 cm or rapid expansion occurs (>1 cm/year), and mortality upon rupture exceeds 80%. Doxycycline (MMP inhibition), statins (anti-inflammatory/ROS reduction), and beta-blockers (mitigation of haemodynamic load) are the main pharmacological intervention targets.

## Key Pathways

| Pathway | Key Molecule/Mechanism | Clinical Outcome |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| ECM degradation | Activation of MMP-9, MMP-2 → degradation of elastin/collagen | Decreased aortic wall strength, diameter expansion |
| Chronic inflammation | Macrophage activation, TNF-α, ROS generation | VSMC apoptosis, sustained transmural inflammation |
| VSMC loss | Accelerated apoptosis · decreased proliferation | Weakening of the media |
| Haemodynamic stress | Turbulent blood flow, transmitted intra-abdominal pressure | Increased wall stress, ILT formation |
| Oxidative stress | ROS → NF-κB activation | Promotes MMP transcription, VSMC damage |
| Intraluminal thrombus formation | Platelet activation, fibrin accumulation | ILT growth, wall nutrient deprivation |
| Statin effect | NF-κB inhibition, ROS reduction, downregulation of MMP | Suppression of progression |

## Drug Targets

- **Doxycycline**: direct inhibition of MMP-2/9 → preserves ECM, confirmed to inhibit AAA progression in animal models
- **Statins**: pleiotropic anti-inflammatory effect, NF-κB inhibition and ROS reduction, VSMC protection
- **Beta-blockers (e.g. propranolol)**: decreases systolic blood pressure and ΔP/Δt → reduces wall stress
- **ACE inhibitors/ARBs**: inhibits angiotensin II-mediated MMP induction, blood pressure management
- **Antiplatelet/anticoagulant agents**: inhibits ILT expansion

## mrgsolve Model (ODE Model)

- **Compartment structure**: doxycycline · statin · propranolol, each with 2–3-compartment PK + 10 PD compartments for macrophages · TNF-α · ROS · MMP-9 · MMP-2 · elastin · collagen · VSMC · ILT · aortic diameter
- **Key treatment scenarios**: ① untreated natural history, ② doxycycline alone, ③ statin alone, ④ doxycycline + statin combination, ⑤ beta-blocker + statin, ⑥ triple combination therapy
- **Calibration/rationale**: parameters referenced from the ADAM study (beta-blockers), statin observational cohorts, and the Longo et al. MMP-9 animal model

## Shiny Dashboard

Consists of a patient profile settings tab (age, initial diameter, risk factors), a drug PK concentration-time curve tab, an aortic diameter change tab, an MMP/ECM dynamics tab, a treatment scenario comparison tab, and a rupture risk/biomarker tab, with sliders allowing real-time adjustment of dose and dosing interval.

## Usage

```r
library(mrgsolve)
mod <- mread("aaa_mrgsolve_model.R")
out <- mrgsim(mod, end = 365)
plot(out)
# Shiny dashboard:
shiny::runApp("aaa_shiny_app.R")
```
