# X-linked Adrenoleukodystrophy (X-ALD) — References
### X-linked Adrenoleukodystrophy · References for the QSP model

**PMID verification method.** The PMID of every entry below was looked up through NCBI E-utilities (`esearch` + `esummary`)
and **checked one by one to confirm that the author, journal, year, and title really do match**. Not one PMID here was
written from memory (two PMIDs jotted down without checking while drafting turned out to be
entirely different papers — one a *Cryptococcus* pheromone gene, the other a TIPS hepatic-perfusion CT — and
from that point on every one was looked up). Citations that were not verified were not put into this document.

The **[model]** marker on each entry indicates which parameter, structure, or
anchor of `xald_mrgsolve_model_en.R` that reference supports.

## 1. The gene · the ALDP protein · the mutation spectrum

1. Mosser J, et al. Putative X-linked adrenoleukodystrophy gene shares unexpected homology with ABC transporters. **Nature** 1993.
   <https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8441467/>
   — identification of *ABCD1* as the causative gene. [model] the `ABCD1`→`ALDP` structure, `MUTRES` (residual transport function).

2. Kemp S, et al. ABCD1 mutations and the X-linked adrenoleukodystrophy mutation database: role in diagnosis and clinical correlations. **Hum Mutat** 2001.
   <https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11748843/>
   — the mutation spectrum and the **absence of a genotype–phenotype correlation**. [model] the basis for separating
   `MUTRES` from the determination of phenotype and giving `SUSC` the deciding vote.

3. Fu H, et al. A Novel Missense Variant of the ABCD1 Gene in X-Linked Adrenoleukodystrophy in a Chinese Family. **Mol Genet Genomic Med** 2025.
   <https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41041882/>
   — the variability of residual function in missense mutations. [model] the possibility of setting `MUTRES` > 0.

4. Pujol A, et al. Functional overlap between ABCD1 (ALD) and ABCD2 (ALDR) transporters: a therapeutic target for X-adrenoleukodystrophy. **Hum Mol Genet** 2004.
   <https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15489218/>
   — the partial functional redundancy of ABCD2. [model] `ABCD2F`, `FABCD2`, and the structure in which ABCD2 contributes
   **only when induced, not at baseline** (`rect(ABCD2F-1)`).

## 13. Summary of parameter sources (LIT / FIT / STRUCT)

| Category | Meaning | Examples |
|------|-----|-----|
| **LIT** | a value taken directly from the literature | normal and patient plasma C26:0, the busulfan AUC target, the cortisol half-life |
| **FIT** | a value fitted to an anchor by coordinate-wise iteration in `xald_calibrate()` | `KELONG`, `KOMEG`, `KLPC`, `KADMD`, `KIGN`, `KMGD50`, `KIERU`, `KMGREP`, `KAXDSC` |
| **STRUCT** | a structural choice with no direct measurement (stated as such) | `KAMP50`/`HAMP` (amplification saturation), `KSPREAD` (spread of the lesion front), `HSUSC` (the susceptibility Hill), `FMGL`, the `KBRSYN`/`KINBR` partition ratio |

**One relationship the model is explicitly empirical about.** The relationship between the Loes score and the demyelinated fraction
(`LOESMAX * hup(DEMYF, KLOES50, HLOES)`) is not a mechanistic equation but the **calibration curve of a
reading scale**. Loes is a regionally weighted summed score, so its relationship to volume fraction is not linear, and
it has to be flat in the low range if a patient with pure AMN is not to acquire a cerebral score. This one place is not hidden;
it is flagged here. (With the exponential saturation curve that was tried first, Loes exceeded 1 point at 1% demyelination
and every AMN patient received a cerebral lesion score, so it was replaced with a Hill function.)

## 14. What was not put into this document

- **Unverified PMIDs**: not one.
- **The detailed event counts of the leriglitazone ADVANCE trial**: that the primary endpoint was not met is certain (reference 5 above,
  Köhler 2023, *Lancet Neurol* — PMID 36681445), but the exact numerator and denominator for the number of cerebral lesions
  were not carried over without checking the paper itself. The model reflects this trial only as **"partial delay, not prevention"**
  (scenarios 18 and 19), and did not use it as a numerical anchor.
- **Domestic prevalence statistics**: not included, because no reliable primary source could be confirmed.
