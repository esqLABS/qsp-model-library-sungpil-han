## Overview
Metabolic syndrome is a cluster of metabolic abnormalities — abdominal
obesity, hyperglycaemia, dyslipidaemia, and hypertension — sharing insulin
resistance as their common basis. Global adult prevalence is around 25%
(over 30% in some countries), and it raises the risk of type 2 diabetes
5-fold and the risk of cardiovascular disease 2-3-fold. The core
pathogenesis is a vicious cycle: excess visceral fat accumulation ->
imbalance of free fatty acids and adipokines (increased leptin, decreased
adiponectin) -> insulin resistance in liver, skeletal muscle, and adipose
tissue -> compensatory hyperinsulinaemia -> multi-organ metabolic
derangement. Treatment is founded on lifestyle modification (diet and
exercise), targeting each component with a combination of metformin, GLP-1
receptor agonists, SGLT-2 inhibitors, statins, and ARBs.

## Key Pathophysiological Pathways
| Pathway | Key molecules/mechanism | Clinical outcome |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| Visceral fat accumulation | Adipocyte hypertrophy/hyperplasia, lipotoxicity (excess FFA) | Induces insulin resistance |
| Insulin resistance | IRS-1 serine phosphorylation, reduced PI3K/Akt signalling, JNK activation | Hyperglycaemia, hyperinsulinaemia |
| Fatty liver and dyslipidaemia | VLDL overproduction, small dense LDL particles, reduced HDL, increased TG | Promotes atherosclerosis |
| Low-grade chronic inflammation | Macrophage infiltration of adipose tissue, elevated TNF-α/IL-6/CRP | Worsens insulin resistance |
| RAAS/sympathetic activation | Increased angiotensin II, sympathetic overactivity | Raised blood pressure, kidney damage |
| AMPK pathway suppression | Reduced activity of the AMPK energy sensor | Reduced fatty acid oxidation, increased gluconeogenesis |
| Adipokine imbalance | Leptin resistance, reduced adiponectin | Dysregulated appetite and energy balance |

## Key Drug Targets
- **Metformin**: inhibits hepatic gluconeogenesis (AMPK activation), improves insulin sensitivity (first-line)
- **GLP-1 receptor agonists** (semaglutide, liraglutide): stimulate insulin secretion, suppress glucagon, reduce body weight, provide cardiovascular protection
- **SGLT-2 inhibitors** (empagliflozin, dapagliflozin): inhibit renal glucose reabsorption, reduce weight and blood pressure, provide cardiorenal protection
- **Statins** (atorvastatin, rosuvastatin): inhibit HMG-CoA reductase -> reduce LDL-C, reduce cardiovascular risk
- **ARBs** (valsartan, irbesartan): block the angiotensin II receptor, improve blood pressure and insulin resistance
- **Pioglitazone** (TZD/PPARγ agonist): improves peripheral insulin sensitivity, increases adiponectin

## mrgsolve Model (ODE Model)
- **Compartment structure**: drug PK compartments (metformin 3-compartment,
  GLP-1 agonist 2-compartment, SGLT-2 inhibitor 2-compartment, statin
  2-compartment, ARB 2-compartment) + disease PD compartments (glucose,
  insulin, GLP-1, visceral fat, subcutaneous fat, leptin, adiponectin,
  VLDL-C, LDL-C, HDL-C, TG, TNF-α, IL-6, IL-1β, CRP, angiotensin II, MAP,
  AMPK)
- **Key treatment scenarios**: ① lifestyle (no drug), ② metformin alone, ③
  GLP-1 receptor agonist alone, ④ metformin + GLP-1 combination, ⑤ SGLT-2
  inhibitor + metformin, ⑥ multi-target metabolic combination (metformin +
  GLP-1 + statin + ARB)
- **Calibration/basis**: based on data from major cardiovascular outcome
  trials — UKPDS (metformin), LEADER (liraglutide), EMPA-REG OUTCOME,
  SUSTAIN-6 (semaglutide)

## Shiny Dashboard
Consists of tabs for patient profile input (weight, waist circumference,
blood pressure, HbA1c, lipids, baseline cardiovascular risk), drug PK
concentration trends, key PD biomarkers (glucose, insulin, lipids, weight,
CRP), clinical endpoints (predicted achievement of HbA1c/blood
pressure/LDL-C/weight targets), treatment scenario comparison, and
cardiovascular risk reduction visualisation.

## Usage

plot(out)
# Shiny dashboard:
shiny::runApp("ms_shiny_app.R")
