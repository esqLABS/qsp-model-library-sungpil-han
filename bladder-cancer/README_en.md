## Overview

Bladder cancer (urothelial carcinoma) is a malignant tumour arising from the urothelial cells of the bladder, occurring about 3-4 times more often in men than in women, with approximately 570,000 new cases diagnosed worldwide each year. It is classified into non-muscle-invasive (NMIBC: Ta/T1/CIS), muscle-invasive (MIBC: T2-T4), and metastatic (mUBC) disease. The main molecular pathologies are FGFR3 mutation (~60% of low-grade NMIBC), TP53 · RB1 · CDKN2A mutations (high-grade MIBC), and activation of the PI3K/AKT/mTOR pathway, with immune evasion via PD-L1 expression as a core mechanism. Treatment ranges from BCG intravesical immunotherapy at the non-invasive stage, to GC chemotherapy (cisplatin+gemcitabine), PD-1/PD-L1 immune checkpoint inhibitors (pembrolizumab, atezolizumab), FGFR inhibitors (erdafitinib), and antibody-drug conjugates (enfortumab vedotin, sacituzumab govitecan) at the metastatic stage.

## Key Pathways

| Pathway | Key Molecule/Mechanism | Clinical Significance |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| FGFR3 → RAS/MAPK | FGFR3 mutation/fusion (~60% NMIBC) | Erdafitinib target; ORR 40.4% (BLC2001) |
| PI3K/AKT/mTOR | PIK3CA, ERBB2 amplification | Cell proliferation and survival |
| TP53/RB1/CDKN2A | Cell cycle dysregulation | High-grade MIBC, poor prognosis |
| PD-L1/PD-1 immune evasion | IFN-γ-induced PD-L1 upregulation | Pembrolizumab/atezolizumab target |
| TGF-β/Treg/MDSC immunosuppression | Immune evasion in the tumour microenvironment | Limits BCG efficacy |
| Nectin-4/TROP2 surface antigens | Highly expressed in urothelial carcinoma | EV/SG ADC treatment target |
| BCG → TLR2/4 → CD8+ CTL | TH1 response, direct tumour killing | NMIBC standard of care |

## Drug Targets

- **BCG (intravesical instillation)**: TLR2/4 stimulation → DC maturation → Th1/CD8 immune activation; NMIBC CRR 55–70% (SWOG S8507)
- **Cisplatin+gemcitabine (GC)**: DNA cross-linking + nucleoside analogue; mUBC ORR 49%, mOS 13.8 months (von der Maase 2000)
- **Pembrolizumab**: anti-PD-1; 2L mUBC ORR 21.1%, OS HR 0.73 (KEYNOTE-045)
- **Atezolizumab**: anti-PD-L1; 1L cisplatin-ineligible ORR 15% (IMvigor210/211)
- **Erdafitinib**: FGFR1-4 inhibition; FGFR-altered mUBC ORR 40.4% (BLC2001)
- **Enfortumab Vedotin**: anti-Nectin-4 ADC→MMAE; 3L mUBC ORR 40.6%, OS HR 0.70 (EV-301)
- **Sacituzumab Govitecan**: anti-TROP2 ADC→SN-38; mUBC salvage therapy (TROPHY-U-01)
