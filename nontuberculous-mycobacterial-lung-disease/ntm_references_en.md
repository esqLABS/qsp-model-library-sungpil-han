## Summary — the three "structures" this model took from the literature

| Structural decision | Supporting literature | Implementation in the model |
|---|---|---|
| Divide the bacteria by **physical niche** (not by phenotype) | #20, #21, #22, #70 | `B_E` / `B_B` / `B_I` / `B_C` + penetration coefficients per niche |
| Enter phagosomal pH 5.2 **once only** and derive accumulation and loss of activity **from it together** | #16, #17, #28, #31, #32, #33, #34 | `R_trap` = 151× and the 12.6× rise in `MIC` are computed from the same pH |
| ALIS is **the same molecule at a different address** — it crosses barriers 1 and 3 together | #40, #43, #44, #45 | separation of `KLIP → KMAC` (intracellular) from `KCEN → KPERI` (ototoxicity) |
