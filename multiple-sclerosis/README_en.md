## Overview

Multiple sclerosis (MS) is an autoimmune chronic inflammatory, demyelinating, and axon-damaging disease of the central nervous system (CNS), affecting about 2.8 million people worldwide and roughly 3 times more common in women. Autoreactive Th1/Th17 cells and B cells cross the blood-brain barrier (BBB) and infiltrate CNS white matter, breaking down myelin and causing relapsing-remitting or progressive neurological disability. Core pathogenic mechanisms include neuroinflammation driven by the Th1 (IFN-γ)/Th17 (IL-17) axis, local antibody production by B cells and plasma cells, microglial activation, oligodendrocyte precursor cell (OPC) injury, and axonal neurofilament release. Key treatment targets include lymphocyte recirculation (S1P receptors), CD20+ B cells (anti-CD20 therapy), natalizumab (VLA-4/α4-integrin), and interferon-β (immunomodulation).

## Key Pathways

| Pathway | Key Molecule/Mechanism | Clinical Outcome |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| Th1/Th17 differentiation | HLA-DRB1*15:01, IL-12, IL-23, IFN-γ, IL-17 | White-matter lesions, demyelination |
| Increased BBB permeability | α4-integrin/VCAM-1 binding, MMP activation | Lymphocyte infiltration of the CNS |
| B-cell/antibody mechanisms | CD20+ B cells, plasma cells, oligoclonal IgG bands | Oligoclonal bands in cerebrospinal fluid |
| Microglial activation | NF-κB, TNF-α, NO, ROS | Progressive myelin and axonal damage |
| Demyelination/remyelination failure | Impaired differentiation of oligodendrocyte precursor cells (OPC) | Falling T-score, accumulating disability |
| Axonal injury | Elevated serum NfL, GFAP release | Irreversible disability (rising EDSS) |
| Deficient neuroprotection | Reduced BDNF, IGF-1 | Progressive brain atrophy |

## Drug Targets

- **Interferon-β (IFN-β1a/1b)**: suppresses Th1, stabilises the BBB; reduces relapse rate by about 30%
- **Natalizumab**: blocks α4-integrin → blocks BBB crossing; reduces relapse rate by about 68% (AFFIRM)
- **Ocrelizumab**: anti-CD20 B-cell depletion; suppresses relapse and progression in RRMS/PPMS (OPERA I/II, ORATORIO)
- **Siponimod/fingolimod (S1P receptor modulators)**: sequester lymphocytes in secondary lymphoid organs; reduce peripheral lymphocytes by 60–80%
- **Dimethyl fumarate (DMF)**: activates the Nrf2 pathway, shifts Th1→Th2 immunity
- **Cladribine**: selective lymphocyte depletion; long-term immune reconstitution

## mrgsolve Model (ODE Model)

- **Compartment structure**: interferon-β 2-compartment PK, natalizumab 2-compartment + receptor binding, ocrelizumab 2-compartment, S1P modulator 2-compartment, DMF and cladribine single compartment; peripheral Th1/Th17/Treg/B cells, BBB permeability, intra-CNS cTh1/cTh17/microglia/oligodendrocytes/OPC/myelin/axons, NfL/GFAP biomarkers
- **Key treatment scenarios**: ① untreated (placebo) ② IFN-β-1a 30 μg IM QW ③ natalizumab 300 mg IV Q4W ④ ocrelizumab 600 mg IV Q6M ⑤ siponimod oral QD ⑥ DMF 240 mg BID ⑦ oral cladribine (cumulative 3.5 mg/kg)
- **Calibration/rationale**: referenced against clinical trial data from AFFIRM (natalizumab), OPERA I/II (ocrelizumab), TRANSFORMS (fingolimod), DEFINE (DMF), and CLARITY (cladribine)

## Shiny Dashboard

Consists of 6 tabs: ① **Patient profile** (setting sex, age, weight, baseline EDSS), ② **PK** (plasma drug concentration-time curves, receptor occupancy), ③ **Key PD metrics** (changes in peripheral Th1/Th17/Treg/B cells), ④ **Clinical endpoints** (ARR, EDSS, T2 lesion count), ⑤ **Scenario comparison** (direct comparison of 7 drugs or combination regimens), ⑥ **Biomarkers** (serum NfL, GFAP trends).

## Usage

```r
library(mrgsolve)
mod <- mread("ms_mrgsolve_model.R")
out <- mrgsim(mod, end = 365)
plot(out)
# Shiny dashboard:
shiny::runApp("ms_shiny_app.R")
```

# Render the mechanistic map
dot -Tsvg ms_qsp.dot -o ms_qsp.svg
