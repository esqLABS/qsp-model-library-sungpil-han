## 10. Iron metabolism and the sign flip


Iron simultaneously drives up the supply arm (ALAS2 translation) via IRE/IRP and the disposal arm (FECH) via the Fe²⁺ substrate and the 2Fe-2S cluster. In XLP, where FECH is intact, the disposal arm wins; in EPP, where FECH is broken, there is no arm left to win.

## 11. Protoporphyric hepatopathy: the single excretion route and bistability
