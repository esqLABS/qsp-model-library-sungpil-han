## The single structural claim this model makes

> **What tears the aorta is not blood pressure. It is the product of two
> terms, and two different organs generate them. A vasodilator lowers the
> first term and raises the second.**

```
        ASI  =  (σ_FL,sys / σ_ref)   ×   (dP/dt max / dPdt_ref)
                └─ the pressure-dependent term ─┘   └─ the sympathetic-dependent term ─┘
```

- The **stress term** applies Laplace's law to the *false-lumen* outer
  wall. Because the dissection plane runs through the outer third of the
  media, the false-lumen wall is only "outer media + adventitia," with a
  thickness of 40% of the full wall (1.90 mm → **0.76 mm**). At the same
  pressure, the false lumen's circumferential stress is about **2.5-fold**
  that of the true lumen. This asymmetry is set by anatomy, not by drugs.
- The **impulse term** is generated by the left ventricle. dP/dt max is set
  by contractility and heart rate, and mean pressure does not enter this
  equation. And when pressure falls, **the baroreceptor reflex raises this
  term.**

So a pure vasodilator lowers the first term while pushing up the second.
The rule "beta-blockade first, then vasodilator" is **a statement about a
product**, and can therefore be calculated.

The second claim concerns the pressure divider, and reduces a well-known,
counter-intuitive clinical observation to arithmetic:

```
      dP_FL/dt = k·[ G_en·(MAP − P_FL) + G_re·(P_dist − P_FL) ] − (P_FL − 9)/48h
      PP_FL    = PP / √(1 + (ω·τ_FL)²),      τ_FL = 0.30 s / (G_en + G_re)
```

As `G_re → 0`, **there is no outflow and therefore no pressure drop**
(false-lumen mean pressure = systemic mean pressure), and because pulse
pressure is erased by the low-pass filter, **false-lumen diastolic
pressure rises to the systemic mean pressure** — higher than true-lumen
diastolic pressure. While the distal stagnation zone clots off and closes
the re-entry tear, the entry tear stays open. This is **partial
thrombosis**, and it is the worst configuration in this model. Nowhere in
this file is there a term saying "partial thrombosis is dangerous" — only
**two anatomical numbers** were changed (entry-tear area, re-entry
conductance).

### Predictions of this model that are not established (read with suspicion)

| Prediction | The model's basis for it | Why it is fragile |
|------|------------------|------------|
| In a beta-blocked patient, **losartan has no effect on aortic growth** (time to reach 55 mm: 2.00 → 1.95 years) | Beta-blockade has already suppressed renin (PRA 1.55), leaving little angiotensin II for the ARB to act on, and the AT1 blockade that does reach the wall reduces Ang II-mediated MMP-9 by exactly as much as it reduces **TGF-β-mediated adventitial collagen** (the model's only negative feedback) | The cancellation could be an artefact of how TGF-β and collagen are coupled. The direction agrees with the Marfan randomised trials' failure to show losartan's superiority, but the model has never been fitted to those trials |
| **Permissive perfusion-pressure augmentation has a net effect of zero** (ischaemic burden 0.74 → 0.37, 5-year survival 0.878 → 0.860) | The same pressure that perfuses the spinal cord also loads the aorta | The model has no time resolution for spinal-cord tolerance. This result should not be used to set the *duration* of augmentation |
| The propagation threshold **TEAR_TH = 0.45** | A substantial share of the acute-mortality comparisons rests on this threshold existing at all | It is a model construct with no value ever measured in humans |

## Why a Python implementation is included (Provenance)

The container this repository was built in has **no R runtime.** Rather
than commit an unverified ODE model, it was judged more honest to
independently re-implement every equation in Python, actually integrate
it, and commit that result alongside it. `aad_mrgsolve_model.R` was
**transcribed term-by-term from** `aad_reference_model.py`, not the other
way around.

The consequence a reader should know: **the science was verified by
integration, and the R syntax was verified only by eye.** Whoever runs the
R file for the first time should be prepared to fix typos.

### Nine defects the integration caught

The Python re-implementation was not decorative. The nine defects below
are ones that never surface unless the equations are actually solved, and
each has been left as a `NOTE` comment at the corresponding equation.

| # | Defect | Symptom |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | The stress → IL-6 → MMP-9 → wall thinning → stress feedback was **unbounded** | MMP-9 at **44-fold** normal by year 3, aortic wall at **0.16 mm** |
| 2 | Baroreceptor resetting was **complete** | The untreated arm drove its own mean pressure above 160 mmHg with **no equilibrium** |
| 3 | The dynamic-obstruction condition was already satisfied at t=0 | The baseline patient was already in **renal failure** before any drug was given (a patent false lumen always has mean pressure > true-lumen diastolic pressure) |
| 4 | The remodelling block had **no negative feedback at all** | A stable chronic state **did not exist** |
| 5 | Tear stress ignored patency | A **fully thrombosed, decompressed** false lumen produced the model's maximum tear stress (because the pressure difference is largest when there is nothing left to tear) |
| 6 | Tear stress ignored tear size | A 9 mm² entry tear gave a **worse** outcome than a 42 mm² one — the opposite of every cohort |
| 7 | A **softplus tail leak** in the malperfusion hazard function | A perfectly perfused organ still contributed 1.05e−5/h → 4× the non-aortic mortality rate → the leak was **the leading cause of death** across every 5-year arm |
| 8 | Spinal-cord ischaemia was described as a lifetime integral | **Certain paraplegia** in every arm |
| 9 | Pain fixed at 10/10 for 5 years in chronic dissection | A **phantom sympathetic cause** of chronic hypertension |

### What the integration verified

There is one point where the analytical and numerical solutions agree
independently. The maximum of the growth rate
`∝ (1 + A·THR)^n · (1 − s·THR)` is `THR* = (n·A − s) / ((1+n)·s·A)`, which
equals **0.426** at n = 1.9, s = 0.85, A = 1.0. The maximum of a 201-point
numerical sweep was **0.425**. In other words, the conclusion that "the
worst false lumen is one that is only half-clotted" is not a picture — it
is **a single derivative**.

### Why closed-loop titration was put inside the model

A nurse raising and lowering an infusion rate to hit a target blood
pressure is **an integral controller**. Leave this outside the model and
each arm ends up reaching a different pressure, making it impossible even
to ask the question "what does a vasodilator cost to lower pressure?" Put
the controller into the equations, and every arm can be compared **at the
same attained pressure**, and the difference that remains is everything
this model has to say. When the controller saturates (nicardipine cannot
bring SBP below 121 at its labelled ceiling of 15 mg/h), that saturation
itself becomes a result.

## Usage

```r
library(mrgsolve); library(dplyr)
mod <- mread("aad_mrgsolve_model", "aortic-dissection")

# Guideline order: esmolol to heart rate 60, then nicardipine to SBP 110
mod %>% param(BB_AGENT = 1, BB_MAX = 900, VD_AGENT = 1, VD_MAX = 15) %>%
        mrgsim(end = 72, delta = 0.1) %>%
        plot(SBP + HR + DPDT + ASI + ASI_FLAP + MORTALITY ~ time)

# Compare against a vasodilator-only arm reaching the same pressure
mod %>% param(VD_AGENT = 1, VD_MAX = 30) %>% mrgsim(end = 72) %>%
        plot(SBP + DPDT + ASI_FLAP + AEN ~ time)

# Chronic 5 years: close the re-entry tear to create a blind sac (the partial-thrombosis phenotype)
mod %>% param(GRE0 = 0.05, D_MET = 100, D_LOS = 50) %>%
        mrgsim(end = 43800, delta = 24) %>%
        plot(D_AORTA + THRFL + PFL_DIASTOLIC + STRESS_RATIO ~ time)
```

## What this model reproduces without ever being fitted to it

- **Partial false-lumen thrombosis is the worst outcome.** The implied
  hazard ratio versus patent is **2.36**; Tsai et al.'s adjusted hazard
  ratio reported from IRAD is **2.69**. What was put into the model was
  not a hazard ratio but two anatomical numbers.
- **A vasodilator alone is harmful.** Nicardipine at its labelled ceiling
  lowers systolic pressure by 33 mmHg while changing dP/dt by +0.3%,
  gives 72-hour tear growth 14-fold that of the beta-blockade-first arm,
  and 72-hour mortality higher than no treatment at all. Every guideline
  says "beta-blockade first," and this model computes why.
- **A small entry tear is a good prognostic factor.** A 9 mm² entry leads
  to stagnation → complete thrombosis → tear sealing → false-lumen
  decompression (9 mmHg) → growth arrest.
- **Analgesia is an antihypertensive.** Fentanyl alone gives systolic
  pressure −11 mmHg, dP/dt −7%.

## Limitations

- **There is no intra-beat resolution.** Pulse pressure and dP/dt are
  reconstructed algebraically. The instantaneous motion of the tear, wave
  reflection, and local shear stress cannot be expressed in this model's
  language — those questions need CFD/FSI.
- **There is no spatial resolution.** The false lumen is a single
  compartment. The real-world morphology in which a proximal patent
  segment and distal thrombosis coexist in the same aorta is compressed
  into a single scalar, "thrombus fraction."
- **The absolute values of TEAR_TH, KGROW, G_re, and τ_FL are model
  constructs.** The direction and ratio of each term are anchored to the
  literature, but their absolute magnitudes were set by fitting the
  outcome.
- **There is no type A dissection, aortic valve regurgitation, pericardial
  tamponade, or coronary involvement.** This model is type B only.
- **This has not been validated for use in patient care.** It is for
  education and hypothesis generation.
