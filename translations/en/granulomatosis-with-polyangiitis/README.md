## Overview

Granulomatosis with polyangiitis (formerly Wegener's granulomatosis) is a systemic vasculitis in which neutrophils are activated by PR3-ANCA (antineutrophil cytoplasmic antibody against proteinase 3), producing necrotising granulomatous inflammation in small vessels. Annual incidence is about 10~12 per million, and the upper airway (nose/sinuses), lungs, and kidneys are the main organs affected. PR3-ANCA binds PR3 on the neutrophil membrane surface to prime neutrophils, and complement C5a further amplifies this, producing NETs (neutrophil extracellular traps) and vascular endothelial damage. Rituximab and cyclophosphamide are used for remission induction, and the novel C5aR blocker avacopan has been approved as a steroid-sparing treatment.

## Key Pathways

| Pathway | Key Molecule/Mechanism | Clinical Outcome |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| PR3-ANCA formation | B cells → plasma cells → PR3-ANCA IgG secretion; LAMP-2 cross-reactivity | Rising ANCA titre |
| Neutrophil priming/activation | TNF-α/LPS priming → ANCA binding → Fc receptor/FcγRIIIb activation → oxidative burst | Vascular endothelial damage |
| NET formation | Activated neutrophils → release of chromatin · elastase · MPO → thrombus formation | Renal glomerular/pulmonary damage |
| Complement-C5a axis | ANCA-bound neutrophils → alternative pathway C3b/C5 cleavage → C5a release | C5aR1 activation, neutrophil amplification |
| Granuloma formation | Th1 CD4 T cells/macrophages → IL-12 · IFN-γ → multinucleated giant cells/epithelioid cells | Nasal/pulmonary granulomas, cartilage destruction |
| Renal involvement | Crescentic glomerulonephritis (ANCA-GN) → rapid decline in GFR | Acute renal failure, progression to ESRD |
| Rituximab B cell depletion | Anti-CD20 → elimination of B cells/memory B cells → decreased ANCA | Remission maintenance, reduced relapse |

## Drug Targets

- **Rituximab (RTX)**: anti-CD20 B cell depletion; non-inferior to CYC for remission induction in the RAVE trial, superiority for maintenance therapy demonstrated in the MAINRITSAN trial
- **Cyclophosphamide (CYC)**: alkylating agent; remission induction via IV pulse (CYCLOPS trial) and oral regimens
- **Avacopan**: oral selective C5aR1 blocker; achieved replacement of high-dose steroids in the ADVOCATE trial
- **Azathioprine/methotrexate (MTX)**: maintenance therapy after steroid tapering
- **High-dose glucocorticoids**: adjunct for remission induction; dose reduction trend with avacopan
- **Mycophenolate mofetil (MMF)**: alternative maintenance therapy (for patients with nephrotoxicity concerns)

## mrgsolve Model (ODE Model)

- **Compartment structure**: includes drug PK (RTX 2 compartments + TMDD binding, CYC gut/plasma/active metabolite, GC gut/plasma, avacopan gut/plasma) + disease PD compartments (naive B cells, memory B cells, long-lived plasma cells, ANCA, C5a, resting/activated neutrophils, NETs, endothelial damage, granuloma index, GFR, BVAS disease activity score)
- **Key treatment scenarios**: ① untreated baseline, ② RTX+GC remission induction (RAVE protocol), ③ CYC+GC remission induction, ④ avacopan+RTX (ADVOCATE design), ⑤ RTX re-induction after relapse
- **Calibration/rationale**: qualitatively calibrated the BVAS remission rate and relapse rate curves based on clinical trial data from RAVE (Stone 2010), RITUXVAS (Jones 2010), ADVOCATE (Jayne 2021), and MAINRITSAN3 (Charles 2023)

## Shiny Dashboard

Consists of tabs for patient profile (baseline BVAS, ANCA titre, GFR, selection of organ involvement), drug PK simulation (RTX/CYC plasma concentrations), immune PD markers (B cells, ANCA, C5a, NETs), renal clinical endpoints (GFR, proteinuria), pulmonary/nasal markers, comparison of 5 treatment scenarios (BVAS trajectory), and biomarkers (PR3-ANCA · MPO-ANCA · CRP).
