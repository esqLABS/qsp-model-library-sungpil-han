## Overview
Autoimmune pancreatitis (AIP) is the pancreatic phenotype of IgG4-related disease (IgG4-RD), characterised by lymphoplasmacytic infiltration and fibrosis. Type 1 AIP (IgG4-related) shows elevated serum IgG4 and multi-organ involvement, while type 2 AIP is characterised by granulocytic epithelial lesions and is unrelated to IgG4. The core of pathogenesis is a Th2/Treg differentiation bias, hyperproliferation of IgG4+ plasma cells, and fibrosis driven by activation of pancreatic stellate cells. Steroids are the standard for remission induction, and rituximab is effective for relapse.

## Key Pathways
| Pathway | Key Molecule/Mechanism | Clinical Outcome |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| Th2/Treg bias | Overproduction of IL-4, IL-10, TGF-β | Promotes IgG4 class switching |
| IgG4+ plasma cell infiltration | Tfh2-B cell interaction, germinal centre response | Elevated serum IgG4 (>135 mg/dL diagnostic criterion) |
| Pancreatic stellate cell activation | TGF-β, PDGF → α-SMA expression | Pancreatic fibrosis/atrophy |
| Bile duct involvement | Lymphoplasmacytic sclerosing cholangitis | Obstructive jaundice, elevated ALP/GGT |
| Exocrine insufficiency | Acinar cell damage, fibrosis | Decreased digestive enzyme secretion, steatorrhoea |
| Endocrine insufficiency | Beta cell loss, decreased insulin secretion | Pancreatogenic diabetes |
| IgG4 immune complexes | Inhibition of activating Fcγ receptors (blocking antibody hypothesis) | Sustained inflammation/tissue damage |

## Drug Targets
- **Prednisolone (0.6 mg/kg/day induction → taper)**: GR-mediated regulation of Th1/Th2 balance, decreased lymphoplasmacytic infiltration — first-line standard for remission induction
- **Rituximab (anti-CD20)**: depletes B cells/IgG4+ plasmablasts → rapid decrease in IgG4 — second-line treatment for relapsing/steroid-dependent AIP
- **Azathioprine → 6-TGN**: inhibits lymphocyte proliferation — steroid-sparing maintenance therapy
- **MMF/MPA**: IMPDH inhibition → suppresses Th2 B cell proliferation — alternative to azathioprine

## mrgsolve Model (ODE Model)
- **Compartment structure**: drug PK (prednisolone 3 compartments, rituximab 2 compartments, azathioprine→6-MP→6-TGN), immune cells (naïve CD4, Th2, Treg, naïve B, GC B, IgG4+ plasmablasts, serum IgG4), fibrosis stages (quiescent/activated stellate cells, collagen), organ function (exocrine/beta cells), cytokines (IL-4, IL-10, TGF-β, TNF-α)
- **Key treatment scenarios**: ① prednisolone induction+taper, ② prednisolone maintenance (5 mg/day×3 years), ③ rituximab 1000 mg×2, ④ prednisolone+azathioprine, ⑤ RTX induction+retreatment upon relapse, ⑥ prednisolone+azathioprine combination, ⑦ untreated (natural history)
- **Calibration/rationale**: referenced against IgG4-RD steroid response studies by Kamisawa, Hart, Khosroshahi, and others, and rituximab clinical data

## Shiny Dashboard
Consists of tabs for patient profile (AIP subtype, baseline IgG4, presence of diabetes), pharmacokinetics (plasma concentrations of steroid/RTX), PD immune markers (IgG4, plasmablasts, Th2/Treg), an organ function tab (exocrine, endocrine, fibrosis index), treatment scenario comparison, and a relapse prediction biomarker tab.
