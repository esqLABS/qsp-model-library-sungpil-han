## Overview

Cardiogenic shock (CS) is a clinical syndrome in which tissue perfusion becomes inadequate due to primary failure of the heart's pump function. According to the SHOCK registry, approximately **80% arise from acute myocardial infarction (particularly STEMI)**, with the remainder arising from fulminant myocarditis, Takotsubo, decompensation of end-stage DCM, lethal arrhythmia, and mechanical complications (papillary muscle rupture, VSR, free wall rupture). By SCAI classification, 30-day mortality reaches **~67% at stage E and ~5% at stage B**, with an overall rate of about 40-50%.

The core pathophysiology is a multi-step vicious cycle: **low cardiac output (CI<2.2) → decreased mean arterial pressure → tissue hypoperfusion → increased lactate + neurohormonal surge (NE 10-20×, RAAS) + DAMP-mediated SIRS inflammation (vasoplegia from excess NO)**. The recent SCAI 2022 update classifies patients into 5 stages from A (at risk) to E (extremis) to guide treatment intensity and MCS application. Treatment is centred on **rapid reperfusion (SHOCK trial 6-year RRR ~30%) · norepinephrine (first-line vasopressor, SOAP-II) · inotropes (dobutamine/milrinone non-inferiority in DOREMI) · mechanical circulatory support (IABP-SHOCK II negative; DanGer Shock 2024: Impella CP demonstrated reduced mortality in STEMI-CS)**.

## Key Pathways

| Pathway | Key Molecule/Mechanism | Clinical Outcome |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| LV pump failure | Myocardial necrosis · stunning → dP/dt↓ → SV<35mL → CI<2.2 | Entry into SCAI C-E, haemodynamic failure |
| Elevated LVEDP/PCWP | Backward failure → pulmonary congestion → ARDS | Hypoxaemia, respiratory failure |
| Neurohormonal surge | NE 10-20×, RAAS · AngII↑, β1 GRK2 downregulation | ↑ myocardial oxygen demand, ↓ cAMP response |
| Microcirculatory failure | De Backer DBI heterogeneity · functional shunting | StO2↓, lactate↑, DO2/VO2 imbalance |
| SIRS / iNOS-NO | DAMP · TNF-α · IL-6 → iNOS → excess NO → SVR↓ (mixed shock) | Late vasoplegia, 30-40% |
| Multi-organ failure (MOF) | AKI (KDIGO 2-3) · shock liver · DIC · ischaemic colitis | SCAI E 67% mortality |
| RV failure (inferior STEMI 25%) | TAPSE↓ · septal bowing → ↓ LV preload | LV under-filling, biventricular failure |
| Vicious cycle of compensatory failure | Acidosis + beta1 desensitization → ↓ inotrope effect | Drug resistance, progressive mortality |

## Drug Targets

- **Norepinephrine (NE)**: potent α1 + weak β1 → first-line choice for MAP/perfusion restoration (2018 AHA/ACC). EC50_α1 ≈ 0.04 µg/min
- **Epinephrine**: balanced α · β1 · β2 activity. Used in cardiac arrest · anaphylaxis/refractory shock. Signal of increased mortality in the Levy 2018 cohort
- **Dopamine**: low-dose D1 (renal/splanchnic vasodilation) · high-dose α1. **In SOAP-II, increased arrhythmia and mortality versus NE → retreat from first-line status**
- **Dobutamine**: β1>β2 → cAMP · PKA · SERCA2a → ↑ contractility. 2.5-20 µg/kg/min. Limited by β1 desensitization
- **Milrinone**: PDE3 inhibition → ↑ cAMP → inotropy and vasodilation. **DOREMI (NEJM 2021): non-inferior to dobutamine**
- **Levosimendan**: ↑ troponin C Ca²⁺ sensitivity (no energy cost) + K-ATP channel opening → vasodilation. Active metabolite OR-1896 (half-life ~80 h)
- **Vasopressin (AVP)**: V1aR agonism. Adjunct in catecholamine-resistant vasoplegia or RV failure with concurrent pulmonary hypertension
- **Omecamtiv mecarbil**: cardiac myosin activator (GALACTIC-HF). Currently indicated for chronic HFrEF; a CS indication is not established

### Mechanical Circulatory Support (MCS)
- **IABP**: diastolic balloon inflation → ↑ coronary perfusion · ↓ afterload. No mortality difference in IABP-SHOCK II (caution)
- **Impella CP/5.5**: microaxial flow pump, direct LV unloading 2.5-5.5 L/min. **DanGer Shock 2024 (NEJM): 6-month mortality 26→18% (HR 0.74)**
- **VA-ECMO**: 4-7 L/min systemic support. **ECLS-SHOCK (2023): no difference in 30-day mortality**; ECMO-CS and EURO-SHOCK also negative
- **Long-term LVAD / heart transplant**: bridge or destination therapy for SCAI D-E or when recovery is not possible

## mrgsolve Model (ODE Model)

- **Compartment structure (26 total)**: 8 drug PK (NE · EPI · Dobu · DA · Mil · Levo + OR-1896 + AVP), 12 haemodynamic/physiological (EF · CO · MAP · SVR · PCWP · HR · LAC · SvO2 · CR · ALT · UO · STUN), 6 mediators (excess NO · TNF-α · DAMP · RAAS · SNS · CUMHAZ)
- **Receptor targets**: α1 · β1 · β2 · D1 · V1a · PDE3 · TroponinC · KATP. Each modelled by receptor occupancy Emax/EC50
- **MCS**: IABP (+0.5 L/min support), Impella CP (+3.5 L/min, LV unload 0.25), VA-ECMO (+4.0 L/min, risk of LV distension)
- **Scenarios (7)**: (1) untreated baseline, (2) NE+Dobu (standard), (3) NE+milrinone (DOREMI), (4) levosimendan, (5) NE+Dobu+IABP, (6) NE+Impella CP, (7) NE+VA-ECMO
- **Calibration/rationale**: qualitative/semi-quantitative calibration against SOAP-II, IABP-SHOCK II, ECLS-SHOCK, DanGer Shock, DOREMI, SURVIVE, and the SHOCK trial
- **SCAI stage output**: derives the 5 stages (A→E) based on LAC · MAP · CO. A simplified Cox-like mortality hazard (weighted by excess NO · CO · MAP · LAC)

## Usage

```r
library(mrgsolve)
source("cs_mrgsolve_model.R")
mod <- build_cs()
sim <- run_cs_scenarios(end = 72)
ggplot(sim, aes(time, MAP, colour = Scenario)) + geom_line()
# Shiny:
shiny::runApp("cs_shiny_app.R")
```
