## Overview

Heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF, EF ≥ 50%) accounts for more
than half of all heart failure patients and is a diastolic-failure syndrome in which
myocardial stiffness (diastolic dysfunction) driven by metabolic comorbidities such as
hypertension, obesity, diabetes, and kidney disease combines with systemic
inflammation and microvascular dysfunction. According to the Paulus-Tschöpe paradigm,
the key pathway runs from metabolic comorbidity → coronary microvascular endothelial
inflammation → reduced cGMP-PKG signaling → titin stiffening → impaired relaxation.
Unlike HFrEF, treatments with clearly demonstrated survival benefit have been limited,
but the EMPEROR-Preserved and DELIVER trials were the first to prove that SGLT2
inhibitors reduce hospitalizations.

## Key Pathophysiological Pathways

| Pathway | Key molecules/mechanism | Clinical outcome |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| Metabolic stress/systemic inflammation | Obesity, diabetes → elevated TNF-α/IL-6/CRP → endothelial oxidative stress | Microvascular dysfunction |
| Reduced cGMP-PKG signaling | eNOS↓, sGC↓ → cGMP↓ → PKG activity↓ → titin phosphorylation↓ | Myocardial stiffness, LV relaxation impairment |
| RAAS activation | Ang II → aldosterone → myocardial fibrosis, sodium retention | LV hypertrophy, edema |
| Natriuretic peptide overload | Elevated LVEDP → elevated BNP/ANP → compensatory response | Elevated NT-proBNP |
| Titin stiffening | Reduced PKG phosphorylation → titin N2B hyperstiffness → poor relaxation | Elevated E/e' ratio, exercise intolerance |
| Myocardial fibrosis | TGF-β → fibroblasts → ECM deposition → LV stiffening | Worsened diastolic function |

## Key Drug Targets

- **SGLT2 inhibitors (empagliflozin, dapagliflozin)**: osmotic diuresis, improved hemodynamics, optimized myocardial energy metabolism; reduced heart-failure hospitalization in EMPEROR-Preserved and DELIVER
- **MRAs (spironolactone, eplerenone)**: block the aldosterone receptor → suppress myocardial fibrosis and sodium retention; evidence from a subgroup analysis of TOPCAT
- **ARNI (sacubitril-valsartan)**: NEP inhibition (blocks BNP degradation) + AT1R blockade → cGMP↑; borderline significant result in PARAGON-HF
- **Diuretics (furosemide)**: relieve congestive symptoms; no direct survival benefit demonstrated
- **sGC stimulators (vericiguat)**: directly stimulate sGC → cGMP↑ → PKG↑ → titin softening; under investigation in VITALITY-HFpEF

## mrgsolve Model (ODE Model)

- **Compartment structure**: drug PK (empagliflozin gut/central/peripheral, sacubitril-LBQ657 gut/central, valsartan gut/central, finerenone gut/central, furosemide gut/central) + disease PD compartments including AngII/aldosterone dynamics, ANP/BNP/cGMP/PKG, LV hypertrophy index, fibrosis, titin stiffness, LVEDP, SVR, IL-6, CRP, GFR, sodium excretion, NT-proBNP
- **Key treatment scenarios**: ① untreated baseline, ② SGLT2i alone (empagliflozin 10 mg), ③ SGLT2i+MRA combination, ④ SGLT2i+ARNI combination, ⑤ 4-drug combination (SGLT2i+MRA+ARNI+diuretic)
- **Calibration/evidence**: NT-proBNP reduction and heart-failure hospitalization rate reduction were qualitatively calibrated against EMPEROR-Preserved (Anker 2021, NEJM), DELIVER (Solomon 2022, NEJM), the TOPCAT subgroup analysis, and PARAGON-HF data

## Shiny Dashboard

Consists of tabs for patient profile (baseline LVEF, NT-proBNP, comorbidity
selection), drug PK dynamics (SGLT2i/ARNI concentrations), cardiac PD metrics (LVEDP,
cGMP, titin stiffness, fibrosis), NT-proBNP/GFR/blood-pressure clinical endpoints,
heart-failure hospitalization risk, comparison of 5 treatment scenarios, and
biomarkers (NT-proBNP, IL-6, CRP, eGFR).

out <- mrgsim(mod, end = 365)
