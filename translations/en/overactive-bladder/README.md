## Overview

Overactive bladder (OAB) is a lower urinary tract symptom syndrome whose main symptom is urgency, accompanied by frequency, nocturia, and urgency urinary incontinence; it occurs in about 11–16% of adults aged 40 and over, with prevalence increasing with age. The core pathogenic mechanism is involuntary detrusor contraction, to which overstimulation of muscarinic receptors (mainly M2/M3), reduced β3-adrenergic receptor signalling, and abnormal ATP-purinergic receptor and NGF-TrkA signalling in the bladder urothelium contribute. First-line treatment is behavioural therapy and pelvic floor muscle exercise, and antimuscarinics and mirabegron (a β3 agonist) are used pharmacologically.

## Key Pathways

| Pathway | Key Molecule/Mechanism | Clinical Outcome |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| M2/M3 hyperactivity | ACh→M3 receptor → IP3/DAG → detrusor contraction | Urgency, urgency urinary incontinence |
| Reduced β3-adrenergic signalling | NE→β3-AR → ↓cAMP → decreased detrusor relaxation | Decreased bladder capacity |
| Urothelium-bladder sensation | Urothelial ATP→P2X3 → activation of afferent C/Aδ fibres | Bladder hypersensitivity, urgency sensation |
| Elevated NGF | Detrusor overactivity → ↑NGF → TrkA → nerve overgrowth | Sensory nerve sensitisation |
| Bladder wall fibrosis | Chronic ischaemia/inflammation → TGF-β → ↑collagen | Decreased bladder compliance |
| Spinal-brain micturition reflex | Overactive spinal PAG-PMC circuit | Failure of voiding inhibition, urgency sensation |
| Relative decrease in bladder capacity | Increased detrusor thickness, ↓bladder capacity | Frequency, increased OABq score |

## Drug Targets

- **Oxybutynin**: non-selective M1/M2/M3 antimuscarinic; reduces urgency/frequency, dry mouth/cognitive side effects
- **Tolterodine**: M2/M3-selective antimuscarinic; improved bladder selectivity
- **Solifenacin**: M3-selective antimuscarinic; reduces frequency and urgency urinary incontinence
- **Mirabegron**: selective β3-AR agonist → detrusor relaxation, increased bladder capacity; no antimuscarinic side effects
- **Mirabegron+solifenacin combination**: combining different mechanisms for additive or synergistic effect (SYNERGY study)

## mrgsolve Model (ODE Model)

- **Compartment structure**: oxybutynin · tolterodine · solifenacin · mirabegron · solifenacin2, oral, 2 compartments each; M3 receptor occupancy (RO_M3) · β3 receptor occupancy (RO_B3) · detrusor activity (DetAct) · bladder capacity (BladCap) · voiding frequency · urgency sensation · urgency urinary incontinence (UUI) · NGF · ATP · nocturia · OABq score
- **Key treatment scenarios**: ① untreated ② oxybutynin IR 5mg TID ③ tolterodine ER 4mg QD ④ solifenacin 10mg QD ⑤ mirabegron 50mg QD ⑥ mirabegron+solifenacin combination
- **Calibration/rationale**: referenced against clinical trial data from ARIES (solifenacin), BEYOND (mirabegron), and SYNERGY (combination therapy)

## Shiny Dashboard

Consists of 6 tabs: ① **Patient Profile** (baseline voiding frequency · urgency urinary incontinence episodes · sex · age settings), ② **PK** (plasma drug concentration-time curves and M3/β3 receptor occupancy), ③ **Key PD Measures** (trends in detrusor activity/bladder capacity), ④ **Clinical Endpoints** (changes in voiding frequency · UUI episodes · nocturia · OABq score), ⑤ **Scenario Comparison** (direct comparison of 6 treatment strategies), ⑥ **Biomarkers** (NGF/ATP bladder mucosal signalling, trends in bladder compliance).
