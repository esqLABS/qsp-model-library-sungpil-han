## Overview
Behçet's disease (BD) is a systemic variable-vessel vasculitis characterised by recurrent oral ulcers, genital ulcers, uveitis, and skin lesions, with high prevalence along the Silk Road in countries such as Turkey, Iran, and Japan (1-300 per 100,000). The core of its pathogenesis is neutrophil hyperactivation and IL-1β/TNF-α-mediated endothelial injury, with Th1/Th17 skewing and Treg dysfunction also playing important roles. Colchicine is the mainstay treatment for preventing mucosal ulcers, while TNF inhibitors, IL-1 blockers, and the PDE4 inhibitor apremilast are used for ocular and vascular involvement.

## Key Pathophysiological Pathways
| Pathway | Key molecule/mechanism | Clinical outcome |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| Neutrophil hyperactivation | NLRP3 inflammasome, NET formation, IL-8 induction | Skin hypersensitivity, sterile pustulosis |
| IL-1β-mediated inflammation | NLRP3 → caspase-1 → IL-1β maturation | Oral/genital ulcers, fever |
| TNF-α/IL-6 pathway | Th1 cell, monocyte activation | Uveitis, vascular endothelial injury |
| Th17/IL-17A pathway | IL-23 → RORγt → IL-17A | Mucosal ulcers, neutrophil recruitment |
| Endothelial activation | Increased E-selectin, VCAM-1 expression | Vascular thrombosis/aneurysm risk |
| Microbiome-immune axis | HLA-B51, gut dysbiosis | Relapse trigger, regional variation |

## Drug Targets
- **Colchicine**: inhibits tubulin polymerisation → blocks neutrophil chemotaxis — first-line therapy for preventing oral ulcers
- **Prednisolone**: broad GR-mediated anti-inflammatory action — induction therapy for acute ocular/vascular involvement
- **Adalimumab/infliximab (anti-TNF)**: neutralises TNF-α → suppresses Th1-driven inflammation — uveitis, vascular Behçet's
- **Apremilast (PDE4 inhibitor)**: raises cAMP → reduces IL-17/TNF — oral ulcers, approved based on the RELIEF trial
- **Canakinumab (anti-IL-1β)**: blocks downstream of NLRP3 — refractory BD, suppresses neutrophil activation
- **Interferon-α**: modulates NK cells/Treg — uveitis, used in some regions

## mrgsolve Model (ODE Model)
- **Compartment structure**: drug PK (colchicine 3-compartment, prednisolone 2-compartment, adalimumab 2-compartment, apremilast, canakinumab 2-compartment), immune cells (neutrophils, Th1, Th17, Treg), cytokines (TNF-α, IL-1β, IL-6, IL-17A), organ-involvement indices (endothelial activation, oral ulcers, ocular inflammation, the composite BDCAF index)
- **Key treatment scenarios**: ① no treatment, ② colchicine alone (1 mg/day), ③ prednisolone alone, ④ adalimumab (anti-TNF), ⑤ apremilast (PDE4i), ⑥ canakinumab (anti-IL-1β), ⑦ colchicine+prednisolone combination, ⑧ adalimumab+apremilast (refractory)
- **Calibration/rationale**: based on the RELIEF trial (apremilast), the Vitale/Hatemi meta-analyses, and EULAR Behçet's disease recommendation data

## Shiny Dashboard
Consists of a patient profile tab (organ involvement, HLA-B51, relapse frequency), a pharmacokinetics tab (plasma concentration-time for each drug), an immune PD indices tab (neutrophil, cytokine trends), a clinical activity indices tab (BDCAF, oral-ulcer frequency, uveitis relapse), a treatment-scenario comparison tab (8-way overlay), and an organ-involvement biomarker tab.
