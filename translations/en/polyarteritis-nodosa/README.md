## Overview
Polyarteritis nodosa (PAN) is a necrotising vasculitis of medium-sized arteries, characteristically ANCA-negative and not involving the renal glomeruli or pulmonary capillaries. Prevalence is about 4~9 per million population, and it is divided into hepatitis B virus (HBV)-associated PAN and idiopathic PAN. The core pathogenic mechanism is a process in which immune complexes (especially HBV surface Ag) deposit in the vessel wall → complement activation → neutrophil/macrophage infiltration → vascular necrosis → progressing to aneurysm/infarction. High-dose steroid monotherapy (non-HBV) or steroid+antiviral+plasma exchange (HBV-associated) is standard treatment, with cyclophosphamide added for relapsing/refractory disease.

## Key Pathways
| Pathway | Key Molecule/Mechanism | Clinical Outcome |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| Immune complex formation pathway | HBsAg-antibody complex, C3/C4 complement consumption | Vessel wall deposition/complement activation |
| Complement-neutrophil pathway | C5a chemotaxis, neutrophil activation/oxidative burst | Vascular endothelial damage/necrosis |
| Macrophage activation pathway | TNF-α · IL-6 · IL-1β, nitric oxide | Amplification of inflammation, tissue destruction |
| T cell activation pathway | CD4 Th1, cellular immunity | Sustains autoimmune vasculitis |
| Vascular necrosis/fibrosis pathway | Necrosis of the vessel media, fibrin deposition | Aneurysm formation/vessel occlusion |
| Organ damage pathway | Kidney (renal failure), peripheral nerves (neuropathy), skin (nodules) | Multi-organ dysfunction |

## Drug Targets
- **High-dose prednisolone**: anti-inflammatory/immunosuppressive — NF-κB inhibition, rapid suppression of vascular inflammation
- **Cyclophosphamide (CYC)**: alkylating immunosuppression — suppresses T/B cell proliferation, for severe/relapsing PAN
- **Azathioprine (AZA)**: purine metabolism inhibition — remission maintenance
- **Antiviral agents (HBV-associated)**: entecavir/tenofovir — treats the root cause via HBsAg clearance
- **Plasma exchange**: removal of circulating immune complexes — adjunct in the acute phase of HBV-associated PAN

## mrgsolve Model (ODE Model)
- **Compartment structure**: PK compartments for prednisolone · cyclophosphamide/active metabolite · azathioprine, PD compartments for immune complex · complement · neutrophils · macrophages · B cells · T cells · cytokine (TNF-α) · vascular inflammation · fibrosis · aneurysm · organ damage (renal, neurological, systemic)
- **Key treatment scenarios**: ① untreated (natural history), ② steroid alone (induction+maintenance), ③ steroid+cyclophosphamide, ④ steroid+azathioprine, ⑤ steroid alone (tapering), ⑥ HBV-associated PAN (steroid+CYC+AZA)
- **Calibration/rationale**: remission rate and relapse rate parameters calibrated with reference to French Vasculitis Study Group (FVSG) cohort data and the Five Factor Score (FFS) prognostic index

## Shiny Dashboard
Consists of tabs for patient profile (HBV status, FFS score, organ involvement), drug PK simulation, immune PD (immune complex/cytokines), vascular damage/organ function clinical endpoints (BVAS), treatment scenario comparison (remission rate/relapse rate), and biomarkers (CRP, complement, HBsAg)

## Usage

plot(out)
