## A. Genetics and imprinting of 15q11-q13

> This section supports the **lesion layer** of the model (cluster 1). The model writes the loss of paternal expression at 15q11-q13 not as a list of several hormone deficiencies but as **a single scalar** (PC1/3 activity), and the justification for that lies in the literature here, showing that the SNORD116 minimal critical region, NDN, and MAGEL2 all converge through NHLH2 on the same convertase node. MKRN3 is the brake on puberty, so the same deletion produces a two-directional effect that **brings puberty forward** while lowering its amplitude (cluster 14).

## Final section · What this list does not cover

Stated so as not to disguise the limits of the model as an absence of literature.

- **There is no explicit state variable for arousal or cognition.** The sleep literature of section K is clinically important, but the model
  does not write daytime hypersomnolence as a state and expresses it only through the airway and hypoxia pathways.
- **The lean-mass effect of testosterone enters only through IGF-1, muscle tone, and the gonadal factors.** The direct androgen
  route to muscle protein synthesis was not written as a separate channel.
- **The evidence level of the GLP-1 receptor agonist arm is lower than that of the other arms** (most of section M is observational).
  The quantitative predictions of this arm should therefore not be read with the same weight as the carbetocin and DCCR arms.
- **Mortality is not quantified.** The literature of section R justifies the pathways, but the model does not compute a probability and
  leaves them only as dotted lines on the mechanistic map.
- **Every time scale below a day has been averaged out.** The actual half-lives of ghrelin, insulin, GH, and LH are in minutes, and
  the model treats them as day-scale pools. It cannot answer a question about 30-minute post-prandial dynamics.
