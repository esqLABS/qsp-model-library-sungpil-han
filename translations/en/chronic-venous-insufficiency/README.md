## Overview

Chronic venous insufficiency is a common vascular disease in which about 25~40% of the adult population experiences lower-limb varicose veins, of whom about 1~2% progress to venous ulceration. The core pathophysiology is reflux venous hypertension due to valvular dysfunction, and the chain of endothelial dysfunction → leukocyte trapping → extracellular matrix remodelling (fibrin cuff formation) causes oedema and ulceration. MPFF (micronised purified flavonoid fraction) and compression therapy are the core conservative treatments, with LMWH added for post-thrombotic syndrome (PTS).

## Key Pathways

| Pathway | Key Molecule/Mechanism | Clinical Outcome |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| Valvular incompetence/reflux | Decreased venous valve function → elevated ambulatory venous pressure (AVP) | Lower-limb oedema, skin discolouration |
| Leukocyte trapping/activation | ↑Endothelial P-selectin → neutrophil/monocyte adhesion | Secretion of inflammatory mediators, tissue damage |
| Endothelial dysfunction | Oxidative stress, ↑ICAM-1 → increased vascular permeability | Protein/erythrocyte leakage → skin deposition |
| Fibrin cuff formation | Fibrinogen leakage → perivascular skin fibrosis | Impaired oxygen/nutrient diffusion → ulcer vulnerability |
| Chronic skin inflammation/remodelling | MMP/TIMP imbalance → skin fibrosis | Lipodermatosclerosis, pigmentation |
| Venous ulcer formation | Local ischaemia + inflammation → skin destruction | Elevated VCSS, decreased quality of life |
| PTS (post-thrombotic syndrome) | Deep vein thrombosis → incomplete recanalisation/valve damage | Severe CVI, ulcer recurrence |

## Drug Targets

- **MPFF (Daflon/Detralex)**: increases venous tone, inhibits P-selectin, reduces leukocyte trapping → decreased oedema, promotes ulcer healing
- **Pentoxifylline (Trental)**: improves blood viscosity, inhibits TNF-α, adjunct to ulcer healing
- **LMWH (low-molecular-weight heparin)**: anticoagulation + anti-inflammatory → prevention and treatment of PTS
- **Compression stockings/bandages (compression therapy)**: reduces venous lumen → decreased AVP, resolves oedema (main non-pharmacological treatment)
- **Sulodexide**: endothelial protection, heparan sulfate supplementation → decreased proteinuria, microvascular protection

## mrgsolve Model (ODE Model)

- **Compartment structure**: MPFF · pentoxifylline · LMWH PK (gut/SC absorption/plasma) compartments + PD compartments for ambulatory venous pressure (PRESS), leukocyte trapping (LEU), endothelial cell dysfunction (EC_DYS), vascular permeability (PERM), fibrin cuff (FIBCUFF), inflammation (INFLAM), oedema (EDEMA), skin fibrosis (FIBROS), venous ulcer (ULCER), VCSS score, and quality of life (QOL)
- **Key treatment scenarios**: (1) untreated (no compression), (2) MPFF alone, (3) pentoxifylline + compression, (4) LMWH + compression (PTS), (5) MPFF + pentoxifylline + compression, (6) mild (compression alone), (7) MPFF + compression
- **Calibration/rationale**: MPFF effect based on the MPFF ESCHAR follow-up meta-analysis (Eur J Vasc Endovasc Surg); pentoxifylline based on the Cochrane review (2012)

## Shiny Dashboard

Consists of 6 tabs: (1) Patient Profile — CEAP classification · baseline venous pressure · ulcer size settings; (2) PK tab — plasma concentrations of MPFF/pentoxifylline/LMWH; (3) Key PD Measures — leukocyte trapping, endothelial function, fibrin cuff; (4) Clinical Endpoints — VCSS score, ulcer healing rate, oedema reduction; (5) Scenario Comparison — 1-year outcomes of 7 treatment strategies; (6) Biomarkers — CRP, D-dimer, quality of life score
