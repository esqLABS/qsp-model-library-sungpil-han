## Overview

Takayasu arteritis is a chronic granulomatous large-vessel vasculitis affecting the aorta and its major branches, with a global annual incidence of roughly 1–3 per million, occurring predominantly in young women under 40. T cells and macrophages infiltrate the full thickness of the vessel wall to form granulomas, and intimal proliferation together with medial fibrosis produces vascular stenosis, occlusion, and aneurysm. The IL-6/JAK-STAT axis and TNF-α are the key inflammatory mediators, and tocilizumab plus steroids are currently the principal treatment strategy. Left untreated, it leads to serious vascular complications such as stroke, heart failure, and renovascular hypertension.

## Key Pathophysiological Pathways

| Pathway | Key molecules/mechanism | Clinical outcome |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| Innate immune activation | Dendritic cell/macrophage infiltration of the vessel wall, Toll-like receptor activation | Early vessel-wall inflammation |
| Th1/Th17 differentiation | Excess secretion of IL-6, IL-17, IL-12, IFN-γ | Granuloma formation, persistent vascular injury |
| IL-6/STAT3 axis | sIL-6R-mediated trans-signalling, CRP induction | Systemic acute-phase response, marker of vasculitis activity |
| TNF-α pathway | TNF-α → NF-κB → intimal proliferation, VCAM-1 expression | Progressive vascular stenosis, tissue ischaemia |
| Vascular remodelling | TGF-β → medial fibrosis, neointima formation | Luminal stenosis, collateral circulation development |
| Hemodynamic changes | Stenosis → PET/MRA-active lesions, blood-pressure asymmetry | Elevated NIH score/vascular activity index |

## Key Drug Targets

- **IL-6R blocker** — tocilizumab 162 mg SC qw: blocks IL-6 trans-signalling, reduces relapse rate (TAKT trial)
- **Corticosteroids** — prednisolone 1 mg/kg/day → gradual taper: suppresses acute inflammation, reduces granuloma formation
- **Methotrexate (MTX)** — adjunctive immunosuppression: provides a steroid-sparing effect that allows steroid tapering
- **Infliximab** — blocks TNF-α, used in refractory patients
- **JAK inhibitors** — baricitinib, tofacitinib (investigational): block IL-6/IFN-γ signalling via JAK1/2 inhibition

## mrgsolve Model (ODE Model)

- **Compartment structure**: drug PK compartments (3 compartments for prednisolone, 3 SC compartments for tocilizumab, 3 compartments for MTX oral/plasma/polyglutamate, 2 compartments for infliximab) plus disease PD compartments (IL-6, sIL-6R, IL-6 complex, TNF-α, Th1, Th17, Treg, vessel-wall infiltration, ST, CRP, PET activity, vessel-wall thickness) for a total of roughly 23 compartments
- **Key treatment scenarios**: (1) steroid alone (natural course), (2) prednisolone + MTX, (3) prednisolone + tocilizumab (TAKT trial design), (4) prednisolone + infliximab, (5) no treatment (natural course)
- **Calibration/evidence base**: parameters calibrated against relapse-rate and PET activity data from the TAKT clinical trial (tocilizumab) and response definitions based on ACR/EULAR guidelines

## Shiny Dashboard

Consists of 6 or more tabs, including patient profile (age, sex, disease subtype, baseline NIH score), PK visualisation (plasma concentration-time curves for each drug), inflammatory PD indices (time series of CRP, IL-6, PET activity), vascular structure indices (vessel-wall thickness, degree of stenosis), treatment scenario comparison (relapse rate for monotherapy vs combination therapy), and a biomarker panel (Th1/Th17/Treg balance).
