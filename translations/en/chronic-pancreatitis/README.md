## Overview

Chronic pancreatitis is a progressive disease in which repeated inflammatory injury causes irreversible fibrosis of the pancreatic parenchyma; alcohol is the main cause in Western countries, and idiopathic disease predominates in Asia. Worldwide prevalence is estimated at about 50 per 100,000 population, and pain, exocrine insufficiency (steatorrhoea), and endocrine insufficiency (diabetes) are the three major clinical outcomes. Pancreatic stellate cell (PSC) activation is the key driver of fibrosis; pancreatic enzyme replacement therapy (PERT) corrects malabsorption, and opioids/gabapentin are used for pain management.

## Key Pathways

| Pathway | Key Molecule/Mechanism | Clinical Outcome |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| Premature intracellular trypsin activation | Lysosomal instability → autodigestion | Repeated acute exacerbation |
| Pancreatic stellate cell (PSC) activation | TGF-β, IL-6 → PSC activation → excess collagen | Fibrosis of the pancreatic parenchyma |
| Neural sensitisation | TNF-α, IL-6 → peripheral/central nerve hypersensitivity | Chronic visceral pain |
| Exocrine insufficiency | Acinar cell loss, main pancreatic duct stricture | Steatorrhoea, malnutrition |
| Endocrine insufficiency | Fibrotic infiltration of beta cells | Pancreatogenic diabetes (T3cDM) |
| Oxidative stress | ROS accumulation → acinar cell damage | Cell death, sustained inflammation |
| Ductal hypertension/stones | Mucus plugs · pancreatic stones → duct obstruction | Worsened pain, risk of infection |

## Drug Targets

- **Pancreatic enzyme replacement (PERT — pancrelipase/lipase)**: corrects exocrine insufficiency → improves fat absorption, restores nutritional status
- **Opioids**: central/peripheral mu receptors → decreased visceral pain, with accompanying addiction risk
- **Gabapentin/pregabalin**: voltage-dependent Ca²⁺ channel (α2δ) inhibition → relieves central nerve sensitisation
- **Antifibrotic agents (pirfenidone/losartan)**: TGF-β signal inhibition → decreased PSC activation/collagen synthesis (investigational)
- **Antioxidant supplements**: selenium · vitamin C/E · methionine → decreased ROS (EUROPAC-2 and others)

## mrgsolve Model (ODE Model)

- **Compartment structure**: opioid (gut/central/peripheral, 2 compartments) + PERT duodenal compartment PK + PD compartments for TNF-α, IL-6, TGF-β, ROS, PSC, fibrosis index (FIB), exocrine function (EXO), beta cells (BETA), blood glucose (GLUC), peripheral nerve sensitisation (pSENS), central nerve sensitisation (cSENS), and pain score
- **Key treatment scenarios**: (1) untreated natural history, (2) PERT alone, (3) opioid + gabapentin combination, (4) antifibrotic treatment (pirfenidone/losartan), (5) PERT + analgesia + antifibrotic combination therapy
- **Calibration/rationale**: PERT effect based on Lévy P et al. (Gut 2012); pain management scenarios based on USPS panel recommendations (Pancreas 2020)

## Shiny Dashboard

Consists of 6 tabs: (1) Patient Profile — aetiology · severity · residual pancreatic function settings; (2) PK tab — opioid plasma concentration/PERT duodenal concentration; (3) Key PD Measures — fibrosis index, TNF-α, TGF-β, ROS; (4) Clinical Endpoints — pain score, fat absorption rate, blood glucose; (5) Scenario Comparison — 2-year outcomes of 5 treatment strategies; (6) Biomarkers — exocrine function (faecal elastase), beta cell function, serum amylase/lipase
