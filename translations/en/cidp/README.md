## Overview

CIDP is a progressive/relapsing neuropathy in which the peripheral nerve myelin sheath is destroyed by autoimmune mechanisms, occurring in about 1~8 per 100,000 population. Pathogenic autoantibodies (anti-nodal/paranodal, anti-contactin, etc.) act together with T cell-mediated inflammation to attack Schwann cells and myelin, and accumulating axonal damage leads to irreversible disability. IVIG (intravenous immunoglobulin) is the first-line standard treatment, with steroids, plasma exchange, and rituximab as alternatives, and the FcRn inhibitor efgartigimod is the most recently approved treatment.

## Key Pathways

| Pathway | Key Molecule/Mechanism | Clinical Outcome |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| Autoantibody production/complement activation | Pathogenic IgG → complement pathway → membrane attack complex | Myelin damage, nerve conduction block |
| T cell (Th1/Th17) infiltration | IFN-γ, IL-17 → ↑endothelial permeability → endoneurial infiltration | Demyelination, oedema |
| FcRn-mediated IgG homeostasis | ↑FcRn → prolonged half-life of pathogenic IgG | Sustained autoantibodies |
| Myelin damage/loss | Schwann cell damage → segmental myelin loss | Decreased nerve conduction velocity (NCV) |
| Suppression of remyelination | Sustained inflammation → suppression of Schwann cell differentiation | Progression of irreversible disability |
| Axonal degeneration | Chronic demyelination → axonal ischaemia/energy depletion | Decreased muscle strength, atrophy |
| Nerve fibre damage marker | Elevated NfL (serum neurofilament light chain) | Monitoring of disease activity/treatment response |

## Drug Targets

- **IVIG (intravenous immunoglobulin)**: FcγR blockade, Fc-FcRn competition → promotes autoantibody degradation, increases Treg, suppresses complement
- **Corticosteroids (prednisolone)**: suppresses T cells/B cells, decreases blood-nerve barrier permeability
- **Plasma exchange (PLEX)**: direct removal of autoantibodies/complement
- **Rituximab (RTX)**: targets CD20 → B cell depletion → decreased antibody production (refractory CIDP)
- **Efgartigimod**: FcRn blockade → shortens the half-life of total IgG (including autoantibodies) (ADHERE trial)

## mrgsolve Model (ODE Model)

- **Compartment structure**: IVIG (central/peripheral, 2 compartments) + corticosteroid PK + rituximab (RTX plasma/CD20 binding) + efgartigimod PK + PLEX effect compartment + PD compartments for Th1, Th17, Treg, B cells, plasma cells, macrophages, complement, pathogenic autoantibody, demyelination, remyelination, axonal density, NfL, NCV, and INCAT disability score
- **Key treatment scenarios**: (1) untreated, (2) IVIG 2 g/kg q4 weeks, (3) prednisolone taper, (4) plasma exchange + IVIG maintenance, (5) two doses of rituximab, (6) two cycles of efgartigimod
- **Calibration/rationale**: IVIG response based on the PATH trial (NEJM 2017), efgartigimod based on the ADHERE trial (Lancet Neurol 2023)

## Shiny Dashboard

Consists of 6 tabs: (1) Patient Profile — onset pattern (relapsing-remitting/progressive) · baseline INCAT score settings; (2) PK tab — plasma concentrations of IVIG/RTX/efgartigimod; (3) Key PD Measures — autoantibodies, complement, Th1/Th17/Treg; (4) Clinical Endpoints — INCAT disability score, NCV, NfL; (5) Scenario Comparison — 1-year outcomes of 6 treatment strategies; (6) Biomarkers — serum NfL, demyelination index, remyelination progress
