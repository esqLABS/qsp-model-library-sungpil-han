## Overview

Dyslipidemia is defined by abnormalities of lipid homeostasis — elevated LDL cholesterol, reduced HDL, and elevated triglycerides — and is a core risk factor for atherosclerosis and cardiovascular disease. About 40% of adults worldwide have dyslipidemia, and high LDL cholesterol raises myocardial infarction risk 2- to 3-fold. The core pathophysiology involves hepatic HMG-CoA reductase activity, regulation of LDL receptor (LDLR) expression, LDLR degradation by PCSK9, and homeostatic abnormalities in the reverse cholesterol transport (RCT) pathway. Statins, ezetimibe, PCSK9 inhibitors, and inclisiran are the principal treatments.

## Key Pathways

| Pathway | Key Molecule/Mechanism | Clinical Outcome |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| HMG-CoA reductase pathway | Hepatic cholesterol synthesis → VLDL secretion | Elevated LDL-C |
| LDL receptor pathway | LDLR → hepatic LDL uptake → degradation by PCSK9 | Determines circulating LDL-C level |
| PCSK9 pathway | PCSK9 → promotes lysosomal LDLR degradation | Elevated LDL-C, increased cardiovascular risk |
| Reverse cholesterol transport | ApoA1/HDL → peripheral cholesterol → hepatic excretion | HDL protective mechanism |
| Intestinal cholesterol absorption | NPC1L1 → absorption of dietary/biliary cholesterol | Contributes to LDL-C |
| Atherosclerosis progression | Oxidised LDL → macrophage foam cells → plaque | Myocardial infarction, stroke |
| Triglyceride pathway | Excess VLDL secretion, reduced LPL → elevated TG | Pancreatitis, residual risk |

## Drug Targets

- **Statins** (atorvastatin, rosuvastatin): inhibit HMG-CoA reductase → reduce hepatic cholesterol synthesis, increase LDLR expression
- **Ezetimibe**: inhibits NPC1L1 → suppresses intestinal cholesterol absorption
- **PCSK9 inhibitors** (evolocumab, alirocumab): block PCSK9 binding → protect LDLR → an additional 50–60% reduction in LDL-C
- **Inclisiran**: an siRNA against PCSK9 mRNA → suppresses hepatic PCSK9 synthesis (subcutaneous injection twice yearly)
- **Fibrates**: activate PPARα → reduce TG, increase HDL

## mrgsolve Model (ODE Model)

- **Compartment structure**: drug PK compartments (atorvastatin, evolocumab, ezetimibe, and inclisiran, each with depot/central/peripheral compartments) + PD compartments (HMG-CoA, mevalonate, hepatic cholesterol, lipoprotein kinetics, LDL-C, HDL-C, TG, PCSK9, LDLR, atherosclerosis indices)
- **Key treatment scenarios**: ① untreated (natural history), ② statin alone (atorvastatin 40 mg), ③ statin + ezetimibe, ④ statin + PCSK9 inhibitor (evolocumab 140 mg Q2W), ⑤ triple combination (statin + ezetimibe + evolocumab), ⑥ inclisiran + statin (based on the ORION trials)
- **Calibration/rationale**: referenced against FOURIER (evolocumab), ODYSSEY OUTCOMES (alirocumab), ORION-10/11 (inclisiran), and the 4S/WOSCOPS statin trial data

## Shiny Dashboard

Consists of 6 tabs: ① patient profile (setting baseline LDL-C, HDL-C, TG, and cardiovascular risk), ② PK tab (blood concentrations of the four drugs and PCSK9 levels), ③ lipid PD tab (LDL-C, HDL-C, TG, non-HDL-C trends), ④ clinical endpoints (change in 10-year ASCVD risk, LDL goal attainment rate), ⑤ scenario comparison (simultaneous comparison of 6 treatment strategies), ⑥ biomarkers (PCSK9, LDLR, and atherosclerotic plaque volume trends).
