2. THE SEPARATRIX E* AND WHY BELL II -> III IS ALWAYS ~A DAY
   The separatrix E* and the log dependence of the time to fulminant collapse
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   28 wk · formula · B = 18  ->  roots of g(E):
     E = 0.0530   stable
     E = 0.2889   unstable
     E = 0.8603   stable

   necrotic attractor E_low = 0.0530 ; separatrix E* = 0.2889
   local eigenvalue at E*:  lambda = +0.354 /d  (positive = repelling)
   transit time from E* - delta down to E_low + 5% of the gap:
     delta        ln-law pred (h)  integrated (h)  
     0.0005       417.7            465.2           
     0.0020       323.6            371.3           
     0.0080       229.6            277.6           
     0.0300       139.9            187.5           
     0.1000       58.2             101.1           

   200-fold range of delta compresses into a 4.60x range of
   transit time — the signature of a logarithm.  This is

   CAVEAT, STATED PLAINLY: lambda above is the QUASI-STATIC eigenvalue,
   with B and NECa held.  The full system restores two amplifiers the
   reduction drops — malabsorption (E down -> SUB up -> B up) and the
   DAMP term (wNEC·NECa) — so the same logarithm is traversed in hours,
   not days.  Measured in the full 39-state system:
     S5_25wk_formula_abx7_indo        Bell II d10.50 -> Bell III d11.33  = 19.9 h
     S11_28wk_formula_severe          Bell II d9.63 -> Bell III d10.54  = 21.7 h

   The arithmetic reason for 'Bell II to III is always about a day', and the
   reason why a marker that rings at Bell II buys so little time.

4. FEEDING ADVANCE IS TWO-SIGNED, AND THE MODEL SAYS THE SUBSTRATE ARM WINS
   Feeding advance has both signs; in this parameterisation the substrate wins
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   substrate enters Ftroph (+, trophic), dB/dt (+, bacterial substrate)
   and the O2 balance (-, demand = 1 + aDEM·FEED/200).
   NOTE, AGAINST THE HYPOTHESIS: we expected an interior optimum.  The
   integration does NOT produce one for NEC — risk is monotone in the
   advance rate.  The price of caution is therefore paid in a different
   endpoint (days to full feeds, TPN exposure, cholestasis, growth), not
   in NEC.  Both columns are printed so the trade-off is across endpoints
   rather than hidden inside one.
   This cohort is EXCLUSIVELY FORMULA-FED on purpose — a stress test, not
   standard care — so the absolute rates are higher than any real unit.

5. EMPIRICAL ANTIBIOTIC DURATION CHANGES SIGN AT A COMPUTABLE DAY
   Empirical antibiotics change sign at a computable duration
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   MECHANISM: what reshapes the lumen is the LUMINAL concentration.
   Ampicillin arrives there (fl = 0.45) and devastates the anaerobes;
   an intravenous aminoglycoside barely does (fl = 0.05), so the
   Enterobacteriaceae are comparatively spared.  Losing C removes SCFA
   (hence the pH suppression of B) and frees niche.  The benefit is
   immediate and the harm is delayed, so the two integrals must cross.
   Run in HUMAN-MILK-fed infants on purpose: the harm IS the loss of C,
   so it can only appear where C exists.

6. THRESHOLD-MOVERS x STATE-MOVERS MULTIPLY, THEY DO NOT ADD
   Threshold-moving drugs and state-moving drugs multiply, they do not add
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   thr_boost  enters ONLY Ktlr  -> a PURE threshold-mover (oral 2'-FL,
              anti-TLR4, recombinant PAF-AH): it moves the bifurcation.
   probiotic  enters ONLY B     -> a PURE state-mover: it moves the
              position along the axis, not the axis.
   HUMAN MILK IS BOTH, and that is the point: it raises Ktlr (HMO, sIgA),
   raises Ftroph (EGF/HB-EGF), AND feeds a commensal guild that lowers B.
   So it is not a threshold-mover to be compared against a state-mover —
   it is a combination therapy, and the two single-target arms together
   are the fair comparator.

   arm                      NEC %     RR        surgical %  onset d  
   formula (reference)      25.4      1.000     1.9         20.3     
   + state-mover only       2.3       0.091     0.4         19.9     
   + threshold-mover only   6.9       0.273     1.2         21.1     
   + both single agents     1.2       0.045     0.0         16.4     
   mother's own milk        5.4       0.212     1.2         21.4     

   RR(threshold-mover) = 0.273   RR(state-mover) = 0.091

   -> multiplicative error -0.021, additive error -0.682
