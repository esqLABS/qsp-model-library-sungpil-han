## What This Model Claims (the structural claim)

Chronic subdural haematoma is usually taught as **a clot that failed to
resorb.** This model does not see it that way, and writes it instead as **a
secreting organ.** A neomembrane grows on the dural side, and its immature
neovasculature filters plasma into the subdural space faster than the dural
lymphatics can clear it. Volume then becomes not a decaying stock but **a
fixed point set by the balance of three terms.**

```
dV/dt  =  J_exudation  +  J_rebleed  −  J_absorption

J_ex  = Lp(N_CAP, N_MAT) · A_mem · [(P_cap − ICP) − σ·(π_plasma − π_cavity)]
J_rb  = k_rb · N_CAP · A_mem · φ_fragility · (1 − H) · MMA_supply
J_abs = (k_lymph·A_mem + k_arach·V) · (ICP − P_sink)
```

The remaining 47 equations exist to supply honest arguments to those three
lines. And **every treatment becomes a claim about which of the three terms
it touches** — this is precisely why the 2024–25 randomised trials disagree
with each other.

| Treatment | What it touches | What it does |
|------|------------|--------------|
| Burr-hole drainage · drain | **STOCK** | Empties the volume. Lp·A_mem·N_CAP·the unexpanded brain remain unchanged |
| Irrigation | **SWITCH** | Lowers the *concentration* of haem in the cavity |
| Craniotomy + membranectomy | **SOURCE** | Removes the membrane area and the septation itself |
| **MMA embolisation** | **SOURCE** | Leaves the stock untouched and cuts the supply → the effect appears as an *integral* |
| Dexamethasone | J_ex | Lowers VEGF·Lp, while harming the patient systemically |
| Atorvastatin | J_ex | Matures the vasculature, lowering Lp while simultaneously raising σ |
| Tranexamic acid | J_rb | Interrupts the fibrinolysis loop (but only reaches the site carried in the exudate) |

### Two Closed Loops

```
L1   haem → macrophage → VEGF → immature vessel → rebleed → haem
L2   immature endothelium → t-PA → plasmin → FDP → clotting failure → rebleed → more fibrin
```

The haem term in L1 carries a Hill exponent of 2. The original intent was
for that single exponent to render the disease **bistable**, steering
"irrigate until clear" toward being a curative rather than a cosmetic
manoeuvre. **After calibration, that intent does not hold** — read the
"Model Report Card" below.

### Atrophy: The Same Parameter Enters Twice with Opposite Signs

`V_RES` (the intracranial reserve space created by brain atrophy) appears in
the model exactly twice.

1. **It buffers pressure and midline shift** → an atrophic patient tolerates
   a larger haematoma and presents later, at a larger volume *(beneficial)*
2. **It slows brain re-expansion**, `k_reexp = k₀·exp(−V_RES/λ)` → space
   remains after drainage *(harmful)*

The same number protects the patient before surgery and betrays the patient
after it. No other term in the model links the two — both are consequences
of a single parameter. Verification results (§5, `csdh_report.py`):

| V_RES (mL) | Time to presentation (days) | Volume at presentation (mL) | k_reexp (/day) | Probability of reoperation |
|-----------:|-------------:|-----------------:|-------------:|-----------:|
| 5  | 21.5 | 44.6  | 0.0776 | 0.000 |
| 20 | 24.2 | 59.1  | 0.0436 | 0.094 |
| 30 | 26.7 | 70.4  | 0.0297 | 0.144 |
| 40 | 31.0 | 82.8  | 0.0202 | 0.159 |
| 60 | 44.3 | 107.4 | 0.0094 | 0.170 |

With midline shift fixed at 8 mm as the criterion for presentation, greater
atrophy **increases the tolerated volume 2.4-fold** (44.6 → 107.4 mL) **and
raises the probability of recurrence from 0 to 17%.** There is a threshold
near V_RES ≈ 20 mL, below which the model cures every patient.

## Model Report Card (what it reproduces, and what it does not)

> This section is written without hiding anything. Every number comes from
> `csdh_reference_output.txt`, generated by `csdh_verify.py` (an independent
> scipy reimplementation of the same 50-ODE system).

### What It Reproduces

**The natural history falls out without calibration.** Integrating from a
thin membrane (12 mL) through neomembrane growth and expansion:

| Item | Model | Reported range |
|------|------|------------|
| Trauma → presentation (78 mL) latency | **33.5 days** | 3–8 weeks |
| Plateau volume | 112 mL | Symptomatic cSDH 60–130 mL |
| Maximum thickness / midline shift | 16.5 mm / 10.5 mm | — |
| Cavity protein | **6.58 g/dL** | 4–8 g/dL |
| Cavity t-PA | 24 ng/mL | Substantially higher than plasma |
| Cavity FDP | 258 µg/mL | Substantially higher than plasma |
| CT density | 50 HU | Isodense chronic phase |

Cavity protein is a **consequence** of the mass balance — not an input
value. At steady-state volume, `J_ex + J_rb = J_abs` holds, so cavity
protein is **bounded** below plasma protein (7 g/dL).

**The MMA embolisation endpoint is nearly exact**: 180-day treatment failure
of **0.044** vs EMBOLISE's **0.041**.

**The best result is the uncalibrated time course.** Embolisation removes
SOURCE rather than STOCK, and the existing neovasculature decays with
τ ≈ 5–9 days, so the effect is an **integral**, not an event:

| Timepoint | Surgery-alone P(reoperation) | +MMAE | Absolute risk reduction |
|-----:|----------------:|------:|--------------:|
| 14 days | 0.000 | 0.000 | **0.000** |
| 30 days | 0.003 | 0.000 | **0.003** |
| 90 days | 0.059 | 0.001 | **0.058** |
| 180 days | 0.153 | 0.044 | **0.108** |

In other words, **the discrepancy between EMBOLISE (180 days, positive) and
MAGIC-MT (90 days, negative) is derived from structure.** It was not put in
as an assumption. A sweep over embolisation timing is also monotonic: day 0
0.044 → day 30 0.089 → day 90 0.124 → none 0.153.

**TXA has a plasma half-life of 3 hours and an effect-site half-life of
about 6 days.** The drug enters the cavity carried by exudate rather than by
diffusion, and is washed out by absorption, so the rate-limiting step is the
cavity's turnover time `V/J_abs` (10–38 days). Cavity/plasma concentration
ratio: day 0.5 0.04 → day 7 0.35 → day 21 0.76.

**The trade-off in anticoagulant resumption is made explicit**: resuming at
3 days gives P(thrombosis) 0.027 / P(reoperation) 0.156, never resuming
gives 0.078 / 0.153 — a sum of 0.183 vs 0.230 favouring early resumption.

### What Fails (and Why)

1. **The haem switch does not operate at the calibrated parameters.**
   `HB50` came back at 0.90 g/dL while cavity haemoglobin is ~4 g/dL, so
   `haem_drive` sits pinned at 0.97 across every arm. The irrigation sweep
   is **flat** (P(reoperation) 0.151 → 0.156 as wash goes 0.05 → 1.00).
   Bistability is present in the equations but not in the fitted region.
   **So this model does not support "irrigate until clear."**
2. **The drain does not match the control**: 0.059 / 0.075 vs Santarius's
   0.093 / **0.240**. In the model, a 48-hour drain only delays recharge by
   about 10 days, and the 90-day risk integral barely registers that
   difference. → **The drain's real 2.6-fold effect cannot be explained by
   fluid removal alone, and requires a permanent state change (membrane
   fusion).** This is a testable claim the model has generated. Attempts to
   decisively raise the fusion rate made the A_EXT–thickness–patency loop
   stiff enough to be practically unintegrable (unresolved).
3. **The benefit of surgery is underestimated**: conservative 0.179 vs
   burr hole + drain 0.152.
4. **Atorvastatin is effectively null**: 0.050 vs 0.052. ATOCH halved the
   conversion to surgery from 23.5% → 11.2%.
5. **Dexamethasone's functional outcome has the opposite sign**: P(good)
   0.264 (dex) vs 0.228 (placebo) — Dex-CSDH showed **worsening**, 83.9% vs
   90.3%. The absolute scale is also grossly off (0.23 vs 0.90). The
   steroid toxicity weighting is not calibrated. Only the direction of the
   local effect is correct (reoperation 0.135 vs 0.152, peak glucose 8.33
   vs 5.40 mmol/L).
6. **In the fitted region, rebleeding exceeds exudation** (plateau J_rb 6.56
   vs J_ex 3.46 mL/day). This **inverts** the "secreting organ" narrative
   above. The fit favoured a rebleed-dominant lesion. Parameter
   combinations forced into exudation-dominance became unintegrable.
7. **The local sensitivity of `MMA_FLOOR` is exactly 0** — because the
   supply term is saturated while PMMA = 1 (a saturation artefact).

### What Sensitivity Says

The sensitivity ranking shows this is a **fluid-mechanics model**: `D50_PAT`
(cavity patency, −13.8%) → `P_CAP` (+12.2%) → `K_RB` (+11.1%) →
`K_SPROUT` (+8.4%) → `KD_CAP` (−6.7%). The cytokine parameters rank low.

## Defects Found by Independent Re-implementation

This environment has no R toolchain, so the same 50-ODE system was
**independently retyped** in numpy/scipy in `csdh_verify.py` and
integrated. In the process, the following **nine** defects surfaced and
were all fixed. Each one had been quietly rendering the model meaningless.

1. **The t-PA production constant was 13-fold too small.** Cavity t-PA
   collapsed from 27 → 2 ng/mL, so **the entire fibrinolysis loop L2 was
   effectively switched off.**
2. **The gain of the protein–oncotic pressure loop exceeded 1.** Trying to
   relax the other states while volume was held fixed sent cavity protein
   runaway to **739 g/dL.** The cause was that fixing volume broke the very
   invariant, `J_ex + J_rb = J_abs`, that guarantees boundedness. → The
   initial-condition assumption was dropped in favour of a **physiological
   run-in** (grown from a thin membrane, with presentation set as t=0), and
   a diffusive protein-equilibration term was added.
3. **The absorption term had no driving force.** Being purely kinetic, a
   3 mL collection vanished in a day, and the model cleared every initial
   haematoma before the neomembrane could even form. → Changed to a
   pressure-driven term (ICP − P_sink).
4. **The model had only a single fixed point.** Every surgery recharged
   back to the untreated volume, so **the recurrence rate was structurally
   100%.** → Introduced a Hill exponent of 2 on the haem term to give the
   loop a threshold (though see §Failure 1 — after calibration this switch
   does not operate).
5. **Irrigation was a no-op.** Scaling volume and solute mass by the same
   factor preserves every *concentration*, so the haem stimulus was left
   unchanged. → Separated out a `wash` factor that reduces concentration.
6. **Membrane area was tied to the current fluid volume.** Draining made
   the just-formed membrane vanish along with it, leaving the thin
   postoperative membrane with almost no exchange area, so no cavity could
   recharge. → Promoted the membrane extent `A_EXT` to a **state
   variable**.
7. **ICP could not fall below baseline.** With no back-pressure, a
   48-hour drain emptied a 78 mL haematoma down to **0.06 mL.** → Made ICP
   bidirectional.
8. **Macrophage drive contained an IL-8 autocrine term independent of
   haem.** Being a second positive feedback with no external input, the
   inflammatory state never switched off (macrophages at 0.63 even at a
   haem drive of 0.12), the membrane always reignited, and **surgery cured
   no one while only MMA embolisation worked** (P(reoperation) 0.0007 vs
   the reported 0.041). → Made IL-8 amplification dependent on haem.
9. **Three numerical stiffness issues.** (a) A `max()` kink in the pressure
   term stalled LSODA indefinitely → smoothed with a softplus. (b) Volume
   appeared in the denominator of the irrigation term and diverged as a
   successfully treated cavity emptied → `V_FLOOR`. (c) **If/else branches
   on state variables** for `A_EXT` (expansion vs fusion) and `V_COMP`
   (loading vs unloading) caused chattering at the switching surface,
   completely stalling the integrator in delayed-embolisation scenarios →
   replaced with a sigmoid blend.

Also verified: **no negative states**, cavity protein peaking at 6.82 g/dL
(held below plasma's 7.0), ICP ranging 4.23–17.83 mmHg (bidirectionality
confirmed), and patency 0.025–0.916.

## Clinical Trials Used for Calibration (calibration targets)

| Trial | Design | Values used |
|------|------|------------|
| **Santarius 2009** (Lancet) | Randomised subdural drain after burr-hole drainage | Recurrence 24% vs 9.3% |
| **ATOCH 2018** (JAMA Neurol) | Atorvastatin 20 mg for 8 weeks | Conversion to surgery 23.5% vs 11.2% |
| **Dex-CSDH 2020** (NEJM, n=748) | Dexamethasone tapered over 2 weeks | Good outcome 90.3% vs 83.9%; reoperation 7.1% vs 1.7%; SAE 6.4% vs 16.0% |
| **EMBOLISE 2024** (NEJM) | Surgery-adjunct MMA embolisation, 180 days | Treatment failure 11.3% vs 4.1% |
| **MAGIC-MT 2024** | MMA embolisation, 90 days | 6.7% vs 9.9% (p=0.10, negative) |

The reference list, and an explicit list of **values the model had to
estimate because the literature did not supply them**, are in
[`csdh_references.md`](csdh_references.md).
