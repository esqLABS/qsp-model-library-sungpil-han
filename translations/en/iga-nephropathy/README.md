## Overview
IgA nephropathy (IgAN) is the most common primary glomerulonephritis worldwide, accounting for about 30~40% of adult primary glomerulonephritis. The core pathogenic mechanism is explained by the so-called "four-hit hypothesis": overproduction of abnormally under-galactosylated IgA1 (Gd-IgA1), formation of autoantibodies (IgG/IgA) against Gd-IgA1, immune complex formation, and renal damage from mesangial deposition and activation of complement/mesangial cells. Treatment ranges from sparsentan (SPARTAN trial), agents targeting BAFF/APRIL, iptacopan (complement C3 inhibition), enteric budesonide (Nefecon), and traditional steroids/ACEi/ARB.

## Key Pathways
| Pathway | Key Molecule/Mechanism | Clinical Outcome |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| Gd-IgA1 overproduction | Abnormal galactosyltransferase (GALNT2/C1GALT1) in mucosal B cells | Elevated circulating Gd-IgA1 |
| Autoantibody formation | Anti-Gd-IgA1 IgG antibody, increased BAFF · APRIL | Immune complex (IC) formation |
| Mesangial deposition | Binding of IC to mesangial receptors (e.g. CD71) | Mesangial cell activation and proliferation |
| Complement activation | Alternative/lectin pathway activation, generation of C3 · C5b-9 | Podocyte damage, decreased GFR |
| Podocyte damage | Slit diaphragm damage by complement/cytokines (IL-6, TNF-α) | Increased proteinuria |
| Tubulointerstitial fibrosis | TGF-β-mediated myofibroblast activation | Progressive decline in eGFR |
| Blood pressure/renin-angiotensin system | Proteinuria, glomerular hypertension | Accelerated worsening of renal function |

## Drug Targets
- **RAAS inhibitors** (ACEi/ARB): relieve glomerular hypertension, reduce proteinuria
- **Enteric budesonide (Nefecon)**: suppresses mucosal IgA production → decreases Gd-IgA1
- **Sparsentan** (dual angiotensin/endothelin antagonist): reduces proteinuria
- **Iptacopan**: inhibits complement factor B (CFB) → blocks the alternative pathway
- **Sibepalimab / Pollokanamab** (APRIL/BAFF inhibition): suppresses autoantibody production
- **Rituximab** (anti-CD20): B cell depletion, selectively used in patients with severe proteinuria

## mrgsolve Model (ODE Model)
- **Compartment structure**: drug PK compartments (budesonide 2 compartments, sparsentan 2 compartments, iptacopan 2 compartments, sibepalimab 3 compartments) + disease PD compartments (Gd-IgA1, autoantibody, immune complex, complement, mesangium, podocyte, tubulointerstitial fibrosis, UPCR, eGFR, blood pressure)
- **Key treatment scenarios**: ① untreated natural history, ② budesonide (Nefecon) alone, ③ sparsentan alone, ④ iptacopan + ACEi, ⑤ sibepalimab + steroid combination
- **Calibration/rationale**: parameter calibration referenced against data from the PROTECT trial (sparsentan), the APPLAUSE-IgAN trial (iptacopan), and the NefIgArd trial (enteric budesonide)

## Shiny Dashboard
Consists of tabs for patient profile input (baseline eGFR · UPCR · blood pressure), drug PK concentration trends, key PD biomarkers (Gd-IgA1 · autoantibody · complement), clinical endpoints (proteinuria · change in eGFR), treatment scenario comparison, and long-term renal function prediction.
