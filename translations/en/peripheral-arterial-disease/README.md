## Overview
Peripheral arterial disease (PAD) arises from stenosis or occlusion of the lower-extremity arteries due to atherosclerosis, and is confirmed in roughly 200 million adults worldwide. An ankle-brachial index (ABI) below 0.9 is the diagnostic criterion, and the clinical spectrum ranges from intermittent claudication to critical limb ischaemia (CLI). The main pathogenic mechanisms are plaque formation through LDL oxidation, endothelial activation, and monocyte infiltration, together with thrombotic stenosis from platelet hyperactivation. The principal drug targets are the platelet activation pathway (P2Y12, COX-1), LDL level regulation, and vascular smooth-muscle relaxation (PDE3 inhibition).

## Key Pathophysiological Pathways
| Pathway | Key molecules/mechanism | Clinical outcome |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| Atherosclerosis pathway | ox-LDL, foam cells, MMP, endothelial dysfunction | Plaque formation, reduced ABI |
| Platelet activation pathway | P2Y12 · TXA2, GPIIb/IIIa | Thrombus formation, acute vascular occlusion |
| Coagulation pathway | Thrombin/fibrin generation, FXa | Thrombus amplification |
| Ischaemia-reperfusion pathway | ROS, muscle ischaemia, nerve injury | Claudication, tissue necrosis |
| Collateral vessel formation | VEGF, FGF, HIF-1α | Compensatory recovery from ischaemia |
| Systemic inflammation pathway | hsCRP, IL-6, endothelial adhesion molecules | Amplified cardiovascular risk |
| Lipid pathway | LDL-C, HDL-C, apoB100 | Plaque growth and destabilisation |

## Key Drug Targets
- **Antiplatelet — clopidogrel**: irreversible P2Y12 receptor blockade (inhibits the ADP pathway)
- **Antiplatelet — aspirin**: COX-1 inhibition → reduced TXA2 production
- **Antiplatelet — ticagrelor**: reversible P2Y12 blockade, component of DAPT
- **Anticoagulant — low-dose rivaroxaban**: FXa inhibition (evidence from the COMPASS trial)
- **Vasodilator — cilostazol**: PDE3 inhibition → increased cAMP, improved claudication distance
- **Statin — atorvastatin**: HMG-CoA reductase inhibition, reduces LDL-C and stabilises plaque

## mrgsolve Model (ODE Model)
- **Compartment structure**: PK compartments for clopidogrel/active metabolite, aspirin, ticagrelor, rivaroxaban, cilostazol, and atorvastatin; PD compartments for platelet aggregation, thrombin activity, LDL-C, plaque volume, ABI, collateral vessels, walking distance, endothelial function, and hsCRP
- **Key treatment scenarios**: ① no treatment, ② aspirin 100 mg, ③ clopidogrel 75 mg, ④ DAPT (clopidogrel + aspirin), ⑤ COMPASS (rivaroxaban 2.5 mg BID + aspirin), ⑥ cilostazol + aspirin, ⑦ optimal combination therapy (clopidogrel + aspirin + rivaroxaban + statin)
- **Calibration/evidence base**: ABI change rate and MACE risk-reduction parameters calibrated against CAPRIE, COMPASS, EUCLID, and CASPAR clinical trial data

## Shiny Dashboard
Consists of tabs for patient profile (Fontaine classification, comorbidities, smoking history) · drug PK profile · platelet aggregation/coagulation inhibition PD · ABI/walking-distance clinical endpoints · treatment scenario comparison (MACE risk, claudication improvement) · vascular biomarkers (hsCRP, LDL-C, plaque volume)

## Usage
