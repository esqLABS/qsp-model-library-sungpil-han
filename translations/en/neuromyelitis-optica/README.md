## Overview

Neuromyelitis optica spectrum disorder (NMOSD) is a rare autoimmune CNS disease in which anti-AQP4 (aquaporin-4) IgG antibodies attack AQP4 on astrocytes, triggering complement-dependent cytotoxicity (CDC) and antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity (ADCC). Global prevalence is estimated at about 1-10 per million, more common in women (~9:1) and in people of Asian descent. Clinically, severe optic neuritis (risk of blindness) and myelitis (limb paralysis, autonomic dysfunction) occur in relapses, and without treatment cumulative disability worsens rapidly. Because IL-6 receptor signalling promotes B-cell survival and antibody production, satralizumab, eculizumab (C5), and inebilizumab (anti-CD19) have become established as standard therapies.

## Key Pathophysiological Pathways

| Pathway | Key molecule/mechanism | Clinical outcome |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| Anti-AQP4 IgG production | BAFF/APRIL-dependent B-cell activation → plasma cells → IgG secretion | Rising serum AQP4-Ab titre |
| Complement activation | C1q → C3 → C5 → formation of MAC (C5b-9) | Astrocyte lysis |
| ADCC | NK cells/neutrophils via FcγR → astrocyte destruction | Lesion expansion, white-matter damage |
| IL-6 signalling | IL-6R/JAK-STAT3 → plasmablast survival, Th17 differentiation | Increased relapse frequency |
| Astrocyte injury | AQP4 loss → disruption of water/ion homeostasis | Tissue oedema, secondary oligodendrocyte injury |
| Demyelination/axonal injury | Secondary oligodendrocyte injury | Accumulation of EDSS disability |
| CD19+ B-cell axis | CD19-expressing cells (including plasmablasts) | Sustained antibody production |

## Drug Targets

- **Eculizumab**: C5 complement inhibition → blocks MAC formation; reduced NMOSD relapse by about 94% versus placebo (PREVENT trial)
- **Inebilizumab**: anti-CD19 → depletes B cells/plasmablasts (N-MOmentum trial)
- **Satralizumab**: anti-IL-6R (recycling antibody) → blocks IL-6 signalling (SAkuraStar/SAkuraSky trials)
- **Rituximab**: anti-CD20 B-cell depletion; long-term maintenance immunosuppression (off-label)
- **Prednisolone**: high-dose IV steroid pulse for acute relapse; maintenance immunosuppression
- **Mycophenolate mofetil (MMF)**: inhibits purine synthesis; maintenance therapy combined with rituximab

## mrgsolve Model (ODE Model)

- **Compartment structure**: eculizumab 2-compartment IV PK + C5 binding, inebilizumab 2-compartment IV PK, satralizumab 3-compartment SC + IL-6R complex, rituximab 2-compartment IV PK, prednisolone 2-compartment oral, MPA 1-compartment; B-cell maturation stages (Bnaive→Bact→PB→PC), anti-AQP4 IgG, C5/MAC activation, astrocyte/oligodendrocyte survival fraction, EDSS, NfL, IL-6/TNF-α
- **Key treatment scenarios**: ① no treatment ② eculizumab 900 mg IV Q2W ③ inebilizumab 300 mg IV Q6M ④ satralizumab 120 mg SC Q8W ⑤ rituximab+MMF ⑥ prednisolone pulse (acute phase)
- **Calibration/rationale**: based on clinical trial data from PREVENT (eculizumab), N-MOmentum (inebilizumab), and SAkuraStar (satralizumab)

## Shiny Dashboard

Consists of 6 tabs: ① **Patient Profile** (antibody titre, baseline EDSS, relapse-history settings), ② **PK** (plasma drug concentration-time curves and complement/IL-6R occupancy), ③ **PD Key Measures** (B-cell, plasma-cell, anti-AQP4 IgG trends), ④ **Clinical Endpoints** (relapse rate, EDSS, MRI lesions), ⑤ **Scenario Comparison** (direct comparison of the 6 treatment strategies), ⑥ **Biomarkers** (NfL, GFAP, IL-6 trends).
