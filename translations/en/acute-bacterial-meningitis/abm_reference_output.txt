  Interpretation — what this model predicts and what it does not:
  · The benefit of giving the steroid at all is reproduced at the magnitude of the trials (see [9] below).
  · But the part that says 'it must be **before** the first antibiotic dose' comes out
    weakly in this model.  The reason is structural: the cytokine burst is 2-6 h wide, whereas the
    hearing·cognitive·cortical damage integrals are on a scale of tens~hundreds of hours.  That is, the
    peak accounts for only a small share of the 14-day integral.  The model is instead far more
    sensitive to **the duration of dosing** (1 day vs 4 days) and to **antibiotic delay**.
  · This is not a convenient conclusion but a falsifiable prediction.  The most likely candidate
    for the missing mechanism is **a threshold in the damage terms**: every damage term in this
    model is first-order in its driver (dHC/dt ∝ driver), so 'a peak 2× higher for 3 h' and 'a level
    1.06× higher for 100 h' do the same damage.  If real hair-cell·neuronal death has a threshold,
    the peak becomes disproportionately expensive, and only then does 'before the antibiotic' become
    arithmetically important.  Putting a threshold in to reproduce the recommendation is easy, but that
    is picking the structure to fit the conclusion, so it is not done here and is left as a prediction.
