## Overview

Pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH) is a progressive pulmonary vascular disease characterised by mean pulmonary arterial pressure (mPAP) ≥20 mmHg and pulmonary vascular resistance (PVR) ≥3 Wood units. Worldwide prevalence is about 15~60 per million, and without treatment after diagnosis median survival is only 2.8 years. The core pathogenic mechanism is pulmonary vasoconstriction and remodelling driven by endothelin-1 overexpression, prostacyclin deficiency, and reduced nitric oxide (NO) production, whereby pulmonary arterial smooth muscle cell (PASMC) proliferation and endothelial dysfunction progressively increase RV afterload. Endothelin receptor antagonists (ERA), PDE5 inhibitors, prostacyclin analogues, and sGC stimulators are the currently approved treatment targets.

## Key Pathways

| Pathway | Key Molecule/Mechanism | Clinical Outcome |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| Endothelin-1 pathway | ET-1 overexpression → ETA/ETB receptors → PASMC contraction and proliferation | Increased PVR, pulmonary vascular remodelling |
| NO-sGC-cGMP pathway | Decreased eNOS → NO deficiency → reduced sGC activity → vasoconstriction | Endothelial dysfunction, platelet activation |
| Prostacyclin pathway | Decreased PGI2 synthase → decreased cAMP → vasoconstriction and platelet aggregation | Increased pulmonary vascular tone |
| BMPR2/TGF-β signalling | BMPR2 loss-of-function mutation → TGF-β dominance → failure to suppress PASMC hyperproliferation | Intimal and medial vascular thickening |
| Inflammatory and immune pathway | Macrophage infiltration · IL-6 · CCL2 → perivascular inflammation | Accelerated lesion progression, worsened prognosis |
| Right ventricular adaptation and failure | Increased afterload → RV hypertrophy → abnormal RV-PA coupling → right heart failure | Decreased 6MWD, death |
| Hypoxia-inducible pathway | HIF-1α → upregulation of VEGF · ET-1, Warburg metabolism | Promotes PASMC survival, luminal occlusion |

## Drug Targets

- **Endothelin receptor antagonists, ERA (bosentan, ambrisentan, macitentan)**: ETA (±ETB) blockade → inhibits vasoconstriction and proliferation; SERAPHIN clinical evidence
- **PDE5 inhibitors (sildenafil, tadalafil)**: inhibits cGMP breakdown → potentiates the NO pathway; SUPER-1 · PHIRST clinical evidence
- **sGC stimulators (riociguat)**: direct, NO-independent activation of sGC; PATENT clinical evidence
- **Prostacyclin analogues (epoprostenol, treprostinil, iloprost)**: IP receptor → increased cAMP; intravenous, subcutaneous, and inhaled routes
- **IP receptor agonists (selexipag)**: selective IP receptor activation; GRIPHON clinical evidence
- **Combination therapy (ERA+PDE5i, ERA+prostacyclin)**: AMBITION · TRITON clinical evidence

## mrgsolve Model (ODE Model)

- **Compartment structure**: 1~2-compartment PK for ERA/PDE5i/prostacyclin/sGC stimulators + ET-1 · NO · PGI2 concentration dynamics module, PVR · mPAP output compartments, including an RV function (EF, CI) prediction module
- **Key treatment scenarios**: untreated natural history, bosentan, ambrisentan, sildenafil, tadalafil, riociguat, epoprostenol, selexipag, ambrisentan+tadalafil (AMBITION), triple combination of ERA+prostacyclin, and others
- **Calibration/rationale**: parameters calibrated from clinical data from SERAPHIN (macitentan), PATENT-1 (riociguat), GRIPHON (selexipag), AMBITION (ambrisentan+tadalafil), and TRITON (macitentan+tadalafil)

## Shiny Dashboard

Consists of 6 tabs: (1) **Patient Profile** — WHO functional class · haemodynamics · comorbidity settings; (2) **PK Profile** — time course of plasma drug concentrations; (3) **Key PD Measures** — ET-1 · NO · PGI2 inhibition rate, changes in PVR; (4) **Clinical Endpoints** — time course of 6-minute walk distance (6MWD), mPAP, NT-proBNP; (5) **Scenario Comparison** — comparison of monotherapy vs combination therapy; (6) **Biomarkers** — right ventricular function, TAPSE, cardiac output.
