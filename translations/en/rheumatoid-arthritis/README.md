## Overview

Rheumatoid arthritis (RA) is a chronic systemic autoimmune arthritis occurring in about 0.5~1% of the adult population worldwide, and is 2~3 times more common in women. The core pathogenic mechanisms are pannus formation within the synovium, persistent joint inflammation driven by TNF-α · IL-6 · IL-1β, and bone/cartilage erosion caused by RANKL-induced osteoclast activation. Serum RF and anti-CCP antibodies serve as diagnostic markers and also form part of the pathogenic mechanism itself. A range of biologics and targeted therapies — anti-TNF · anti-IL-6 · CTLA-4-Ig · CD20 · JAK inhibitors — are established as standard treatment.

## Key Pathways

| Pathway | Key Molecule/Mechanism | Clinical Outcome |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| TNF-α/NF-κB pathway | Macrophage/FLS TNF → NF-κB → IL-6 · IL-1β · MMP cascade | Synovial hyperplasia, pannus, bone erosion |
| IL-6/JAK-STAT3 pathway | IL-6 → IL-6R/gp130 → JAK1-STAT3 → acute phase response | Elevated CRP, systemic symptoms, anaemia |
| T cell co-stimulation (CD28/CTLA-4) | CD4+ Th1 · Th17 → activation of synoviocytes and B cells | Autoantibody production, joint infiltration |
| B cell/autoantibodies | RF · anti-CCP immune complexes → complement activation → FcγR signalling | Complement-mediated joint damage |
| RANKL/OPG imbalance | IL-17 · TNF → RANKL overexpression by FLS → osteoclasts | Radiographic bone erosion, osteoporosis |
| Invasive FLS proliferation | Akt/PI3K · Wnt signalling → pannus formation by fibroblast-like synoviocytes (FLS) | Cartilage erosion, joint deformity |
| JAK-STAT integration | IL-2 · IFN-γ · GM-CSF → JAK1/2/3 → multiple cytokine production | Maintenance of chronic synovial inflammation |

## Drug Targets

- **Anti-TNF agents (combined with methotrexate: etanercept, infliximab, adalimumab, certolizumab, golimumab)**: direct TNF neutralisation; strong evidence for inhibiting radiographic progression
- **Anti-IL-6R (tocilizumab, sarilumab)**: blocks IL-6 signalling → normalises CRP and acute phase response; used with MTX or as monotherapy
- **CTLA-4-Ig (abatacept)**: CD80/86 blockade → inhibits T cell co-stimulation; superior efficacy in RF/anti-CCP-positive patients
- **Anti-CD20 (rituximab)**: B cell depletion → reduced autoantibodies; a major alternative after anti-TNF failure
- **JAK inhibitors (tofacitinib, baricitinib, upadacitinib, filgotinib)**: selective JAK1/2/3 inhibition; convenience of oral administration
- **csDMARDs (methotrexate, leflunomide, hydroxychloroquine, sulfasalazine)**: foundational treatment, first-line for early RA

## mrgsolve Model (ODE Model)

- **Compartment structure**: PK compartments for anti-TNF/anti-IL-6/CTLA-4-Ig/JAK inhibitors/MTX + TNF · IL-6 · IL-17 concentration dynamics, CD4+ T cell/FLS activation module, joint damage indices (DAS28 · HAQ), including a radiographic progression mTSS compartment
- **Key treatment scenarios**: untreated natural history, MTX alone, etanercept+MTX, adalimumab+MTX, tocilizumab alone/combination, abatacept, rituximab, tofacitinib, baricitinib, upadacitinib, etc.
- **Calibration/rationale**: based on clinical data from TEMPO (etanercept+MTX), OPTION (tocilizumab), AIM (abatacept), REFLEX (rituximab), RA-BEAM (baricitinib), and ORAL Scan (tofacitinib)

## Shiny Dashboard

Consists of 6 tabs: (1) **Patient Profile** — seropositivity · baseline DAS28 · RF/anti-CCP settings; (2) **PK Profile** — time course of plasma concentrations for biologics/small molecules; (3) **Key PD Measures** — TNF · IL-6 inhibition rate, CRP dynamics; (4) **Clinical Endpoints** — time course of DAS28 · ACR20/50/70 · HAQ · mTSS; (5) **Scenario Comparison** — comparison of remission rates and radiographic progression across treatment strategies; (6) **Biomarkers** — RF, anti-CCP, ESR, CRP, joint ultrasound score.
