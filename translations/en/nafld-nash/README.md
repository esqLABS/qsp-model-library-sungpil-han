## Overview

Non-alcoholic fatty liver disease (NAFLD) is a disease in which fat accumulates within hepatocytes in the absence of alcohol consumption; its global prevalence reaches about 25% and it is closely linked to type 2 diabetes and obesity. It can progress from simple fatty liver (NAFL) to steatohepatitis (NASH), liver fibrosis, cirrhosis, and hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC), and NASH is emerging as one of the leading causes of liver transplantation worldwide. Its pathogenesis follows a "multiple-hit" model, in which insulin-resistance-driven hepatic fat accumulation, lipotoxicity, Kupffer-cell activation with TNF-α/IL-6-mediated inflammation, and TGF-β/HSC activation together drive progression of fibrosis. Resmetirom (a selective THR-β agonist), approved by the FDA in 2024, and GLP-1 receptor agonists are now emerging as the leading therapies.

## Key Pathophysiological Pathways

| Pathway | Key molecule/mechanism | Clinical outcome |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| Insulin resistance | Impaired IRS-1 phosphorylation → failure to suppress lipolysis, hepatic fat accumulation | Fatty liver (>5% hepatocyte steatosis) |
| Lipotoxicity | Free fatty acids/DAG/ceramide → ER stress, mitochondrial dysfunction | Hepatocyte injury, elevated ALT |
| Kupffer-cell activation | LPS/TLR4 → NF-κB → TNF-α, IL-6 secretion | Hepatic inflammation (NASH grade) |
| Hepatic stellate cell (HSC) activation | TGF-β1 → SMAD2/3 → collagen I/III synthesis | Liver fibrosis (F0→F4) |
| Lipid metabolism | THR-β → ↑ hepatic fatty-acid oxidation, ↓ LDL cholesterol | Reduced hepatic fat |
| Gut-liver axis | Gut dysbiosis → influx of short-chain fatty acids/LPS | Worsened systemic inflammation |
| Adipocyte endocrine function | ↓ adiponectin, ↑ leptin, altered FGF21 | Worsened insulin resistance |

## Drug Targets

- **Resmetirom** (THR-β agonist): activates the liver-selective thyroid hormone receptor β → promotes fatty-acid oxidation, reduces hepatic fat (MAESTRO-NASH phase 3)
- **Semaglutide** (GLP-1RA): reduces food intake, promotes weight loss, improves insulin sensitivity → reduces hepatic fat and NAS
- **Empagliflozin** (SGLT-2 inhibitor): urinary glucose excretion drives a negative energy balance → reduces hepatic fat and body weight
- **Obeticholic acid** (FXR agonist): activates the bile-acid receptor → improves lipid metabolism and liver fibrosis
- **Orlistat** (lipase inhibitor): inhibits dietary fat absorption → aids weight loss

## mrgsolve Model (ODE Model)

- **Compartment structure**: resmetirom oral 3-compartment PK (gut→central→liver), obeticholic acid 2-compartment, semaglutide SC 2-compartment, empagliflozin oral 2-compartment; compartments for body weight, body fat, adiponectin, insulin resistance, liver fat (LIVER_FAT), Kupffer cells, TNF-α, IL-6, TGF-β, HSC, collagen, ALT
- **Key treatment scenarios**: ① placebo ② resmetirom 100 mg QD ③ obeticholic acid 25 mg QD ④ semaglutide 2.4 mg SC QW ⑤ empagliflozin 10 mg QD
- **Calibration/rationale**: based on clinical trial parameters from MAESTRO-NASH (resmetirom), REGENERATE (obeticholic acid), and STEP-1 (semaglutide)

## Shiny Dashboard

Consists of 6 tabs: ① **Patient Profile** (BMI, diabetes status, baseline fibrosis-stage settings), ② **PK** (plasma/liver drug concentration-time curves), ③ **PD Key Measures** (hepatic fat fraction, ALT, insulin resistance HOMA-IR), ④ **Clinical Endpoints** (NAS score, fibrosis stage, collagen quantification), ⑤ **Scenario Comparison** (direct comparison of the 5 treatment strategies), ⑥ **Biomarkers** (TNF-α, TGF-β, adiponectin, body-weight trends).
