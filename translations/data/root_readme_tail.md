## ⚠️ Disclaimer

Every model in this library is a **qualitative / semi-quantitative QSP model for
educational and research purposes**. The models are assembled from the published
literature and clinical trial data, but they have not been independently verified or
certified, and **must not be used directly for clinical decision-making,
prescribing, or regulatory submission**. Parameters and assumptions are
illustrative approximations; fitting and validation against real patient data would
be required separately.
