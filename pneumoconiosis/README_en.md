## Overview
Pneumoconiosis is an occupational lung disease caused by long-term inhalation of inorganic dusts such as silica, coal dust, and asbestos; millions of mining, construction, and manufacturing workers worldwide are exposed, and it is characterised by progressive pulmonary fibrosis. The core pathogenic mechanism is an irreversible fibrotic process in which alveolar macrophages phagocytose dust → NLRP3 inflammasome activation → release of IL-1β/TNF-α → TGF-β-mediated myofibroblast proliferation and collagen deposition. Blocking exposure remains most important, with antifibrotic agents such as pirfenidone/nintedanib and NAC antioxidant therapy used as adjuncts.

## Key Pathways
| Pathway | Key Molecule/Mechanism | Clinical Outcome |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| Dust deposition/overload pathway | Dust accumulation in the alveoli, overload of mucociliary clearance | Activation of alveolar macrophages |
| NLRP3 inflammasome pathway | Phagosomal destabilisation → release of caspase-1 · IL-1β · IL-18 | Acute and chronic lung inflammation |
| Oxidative stress pathway | ROS generation, GSH depletion, mitochondrial damage | Promotes cell death/fibrosis |
| Macrophage polarisation pathway | M1→M2 conversion, macrophage frustrated phagocytosis | Increased secretion of TGF-β/IL-10 |
| TGF-β fibrosis pathway | Smad2/3 signalling, myofibroblast activation | Collagen deposition, lung stiffening |
| Decline in pulmonary function pathway | Decreased FVC/DLCO, increased pulmonary vascular resistance | Respiratory failure, pulmonary arterial hypertension |

## Drug Targets
- **Pirfenidone**: TGF-β signal inhibition · antifibrotic · antioxidant — efficacy confirmed in the IPF indication, under investigation for pneumoconiosis
- **Nintedanib**: PDGFR/FGFR/VEGFR inhibition — suppresses fibrosis progression
- **N-acetylcysteine (NAC)**: antioxidant (GSH precursor) — mitigates ROS-mediated damage
- **Tetrandrine**: calcium channel blockade/anti-inflammatory — clinical studies for pneumoconiosis ongoing
- **Silica exposure control**: dust protective equipment, ventilation systems — fundamental prevention

## mrgsolve Model (ODE Model)
- **Compartment structure**: alveolar dust deposition/clearance compartment, macrophages (resting/activated), inflammation compartments for NLRP3 · IL-1β · TNF-α · TGF-β · IL-10 · ROS · GSH · neutrophils, fibrosis PD compartments for myofibroblasts · collagen · FVC · pulmonary vascular resistance, PK compartments for NAC/pirfenidone/nintedanib/tetrandrine
- **Key treatment scenarios**: ① continued exposure (untreated), ② NAC 600 mg TID, ③ pirfenidone 801 mg TID, ④ nintedanib 150 mg BID, ⑤ NAC+pirfenidone combination
- **Calibration/rationale**: calibrated with reference to FVC-decline suppression data from the CAPACITY and ASCEND trials (pirfenidone in IPF) and the INPULSIS trial (nintedanib)

## Shiny Dashboard
Consists of tabs for patient profile (dust type, exposure duration, pulmonary function grade), dust clearance/inflammation PK/PD, fibrosis progression (FVC/DLCO) PD, clinical endpoints (rate of pulmonary function decline, pulmonary vascular resistance), treatment scenario comparison (fibrosis suppression effect), and oxidative stress/cytokine biomarkers
