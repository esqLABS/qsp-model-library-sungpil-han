## Overview

Small cell lung cancer (SCLC) is a highly aggressive neuroendocrine tumour that accounts for about 15% of all lung cancers, with about 70% presenting as Extensive Stage (ES) at diagnosis. Median survival without treatment is only 2–4 months, and even with first-line treatment (carboplatin/etoposide + atezolizumab or durvalumab) median overall survival is only about 12–14 months. The core pathogenic mechanisms of SCLC are the concurrent loss of TP53 and RB1, determination of the neuroendocrine (NE) subtype by the ASCL1/NEUROD1/YAP1/POU2F3 transcription factors, aberrant surface expression of DLL3 (a Notch inhibitory ligand), and a highly immunosuppressive tumour microenvironment (TME). The high DLL3 positivity rate (~80%) makes it the target of the novel BiTE antibody tarlatamab.

## Key Pathways

| Pathway | Key Molecule/Mechanism | Clinical Outcome |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| TP53/RB1 loss | Inability to arrest the cell cycle → rapid proliferation and division | Highly malignant, rapid metastasis |
| NE subtype transcriptional programme | ASCL1/NEUROD1 (NE-high), YAP1/POU2F3 (NE-low) | Determines treatment responsiveness and resistance |
| DLL3/Notch pathway | DLL3 overexpression → Notch inhibition → maintenance of NE phenotype | Tarlatamab BiTE target |
| DNA damage response | Topo-II poison (etoposide), platinum agent (carboplatin) → DSB | Rationale for first-line treatment |
| Immune evasion | PD-L1/TIGIT/CD47, MHC-I loss, MDSC | Target for IO treatment |
| Cell cycle (CDK4/6) | Rb inactivation → E2F → rapid division | Rationale for trilaciclib myeloprotection |
| Right ventricular adaptation | Pulmonary infiltration → hypoxia → decreased CO | Indicator of clinical deterioration |

## Drug Targets

- **Platinum + etoposide (CE)**: DNA double-strand break → tumour cell death; first-line standard of care
- **Atezolizumab (Atezo)**: PD-L1 blockade; IMpower133 OS HR 0.70 (13.9 vs 12.3 months)
- **Durvalumab (Durva)**: PD-L1 blockade; CASPIAN OS HR 0.73 (13.0 vs 10.3 months)
- **Tarlatamab**: DLL3 × CD3 bispecific antibody (BiTE) → recruits T cells to DLL3+ tumour cells; DeLLphi-301 ORR 40%, mDOR 9.7 months
- **Lurbinectedin**: RNA polymerase II degradation, DNA alkylation; second-line ORR 35.2%
- **Topotecan**: TOP1 poison → SSB; standard second-line treatment
- **Trilaciclib**: CDK4/6 inhibition → G1 arrest → protection of haematopoietic stem cells (↓ myelosuppression); administered before chemotherapy

## Shiny Dashboard

6 tabs:
1. **Patient Profile** — set stage, ECOG, NE subtype, DLL3 expression, TMB, comorbidities
2. **Drug PK** — time course of plasma concentrations for immunotherapy, chemotherapy, and trilaciclib
3. **Molecular Pathways** — DNA damage indicator, BiTE effect, PD-L1 RO, IFN-γ, NK
4. **Clinical Endpoints** — SLD tumour burden, response classification (PR/SD/PD), dynamics by cell compartment
5. **Scenario Comparison** — simultaneous comparison of 8 treatment strategies + efficacy summary table
6. **Biomarkers & TME** — CD8T, NK, IFN-γ, NE markers (ProGRP, NSE), tarlatamab efficacy by DLL3 expression level
