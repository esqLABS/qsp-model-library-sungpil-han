## Overview
Autoimmune hemolytic anemia (AIHA) is a rare blood disease in which autoantibodies bind surface antigens on red blood cells and trigger their premature destruction, with a prevalence of roughly 1–3 per 100,000 population. In warm AIHA, IgG autoantibodies (predominantly targeting Rh antigens) promote phagocytic clearance in the spleen, whereas in cold AIHA, IgM antibodies activate complement C1q to trigger intravascular hemolysis. The core drug targets are the autoantibody-producing B cells and plasma cells, the C1s/C3b complement pathway, and the Fc receptors of splenic macrophages. Sutimlimab (anti-C1s), recently approved, has attracted major interest for directly blocking the intravascular hemolysis of cold agglutinin disease (CAD).

## Key Pathophysiological Pathways
| Pathway | Key molecules/mechanism | Clinical outcome |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| B-cell activation and autoantibody production | BCR signalling, TLR7/9, T-B interaction | Production of anti-RBC IgG/IgM autoantibodies |
| Splenic phagocytic clearance (extravascular hemolysis) | FcγRI/III, C3b opsonisation | Splenomegaly, worsening anemia |
| Complement activation (intravascular hemolysis) | C1q/C1s activation → C3b/MAC formation | Hemoglobinuria, acute hemolytic crisis |
| EPO-reticulocyte compensation | EPO receptor, BFU-E/CFU-E proliferation | Reticulocytosis, bone marrow hyperplasia |
| Impaired tissue iron recycling | Hemoglobin/haptoglobin depletion | Elevated serum LDH, increased bilirubin |
| Syk/BTK signalling (downstream of FcR) | Splenic macrophage activation | Target of fostamatinib (Syk inhibitor) |

## Key Drug Targets
- **Corticosteroids (prednisolone, dexamethasone)**: steroid receptor → suppresses T cells, reduces macrophage Fc receptor expression — first-line standard treatment
- **Rituximab (anti-CD20)**: B-cell depletion → suppresses autoantibody production — second-line or first-line combination treatment
- **Sutimlimab (anti-C1s)**: blocks complement C1s → suppresses intravascular hemolysis — approved treatment for CAD (CADENZA trial)
- **Fostamatinib (Syk inhibitor)**: blocks downstream FcR signalling → reduces splenic phagocytic activity — refractory warm AIHA
- **MMF (mycophenolate mofetil)**: suppresses lymphocyte proliferation → reduces autoantibodies — maintenance treatment
- **IVIG**: FcRn saturation and Fc receptor blockade — acute rescue treatment
