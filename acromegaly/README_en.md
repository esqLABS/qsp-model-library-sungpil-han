## Overview

Acromegaly arises from a growth hormone (GH)-hypersecreting pituitary adenoma (a GH-secreting adenoma), a rare endocrine disease with a prevalence of about 60 per million population. Excess GH secretion continuously stimulates hepatic IGF-1 production, causing overgrowth of peripheral soft tissue, bone, and viscera, along with cardiovascular complications, glucose metabolism abnormalities, and arthropathy. Diagnostic delay averages a lengthy 8–10 years, and untreated disease carries a 2–3-fold increase in standardised mortality ratio. Surgical resection is the first-line treatment, and for residual tumour, somatostatin analogues (SSA), pegvisomant, and dopamine agonists are the pharmacological targets.

## Key Pathways

| Pathway | Key Molecule/Mechanism | Clinical Outcome |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| Excess GH secretion | Autonomous GH secretion by the pituitary adenoma, excess GHRH stimulation | Elevated basal GH and glucose-suppressed GH nadir |
| IGF-1 axis | JAK2-STAT5b activation → increased hepatic IGF-1 synthesis | Soft tissue/organ enlargement, bone overgrowth |
| SSTR signalling | SSA binding to SSTR2/5 → cAMP inhibition, decreased GH secretion | Normalisation of blood GH/IGF-1 |
| GHR blockade | Pegvisomant (GHR antagonist) → blocks IGF-1 production | Normalises IGF-1 despite elevated GH |
| Cardiovascular remodelling | IGF-1 receptor → left ventricular hypertrophy, arrhythmia | Heart failure, risk of cardiovascular death |
| Metabolic abnormality | Insulin-antagonising effect of GH | Diabetes, hypertension, hyperlipidaemia |
| Tumour growth | MAPK/PI3K/AKT → cell proliferation | Determines tumour size/invasiveness |

## Drug Targets

- **Somatostatin analogues (octreotide LAR, lanreotide)**: SSTR2/5 agonism → decreased GH/IGF-1, modest reduction in tumour size
- **Pegvisomant**: GHR antagonist → about 90% IGF-1 normalisation rate; requires monitoring of tumour size due to rising GH
- **Dopamine agonists (cabergoline)**: D2 receptor agonism, limited effect alone but provides additional IGF-1 suppression when combined with SSA
- **Surgery (transsphenoidal surgery)**: about 80% cure rate for microadenomas; low cure rate for macroadenomas
- **Radiotherapy**: gradually lowers GH; carries risk of hypopituitarism

## mrgsolve Model (ODE Model)

- **Compartment structure**: SSA (octreotide) 2-compartment PK + pegvisomant 2-compartment PK + PD compartments for SSTR binding/GHR blockade + adenoma GH secretion, plasma GH, STAT5b activity, hepatic IGF-1, plasma IGF-1, tumour volume, left ventricular hypertrophy, blood glucose, joint score, and others
- **Key treatment scenarios**: ① untreated, ② octreotide LAR alone, ③ pegvisomant alone, ④ SSA + pegvisomant combination, ⑤ postoperative residual adenoma with SSA, ⑥ addition of a dopamine agonist
- **Calibration/rationale**: parameters referenced from the ACROSTUDY registry (pegvisomant), the PRIMARYS clinical trial (lanreotide), and the Colao et al. meta-analysis

## Shiny Dashboard

Consists of a patient profile tab (sex, age, tumour size), a GH/IGF-1 PK-PD dynamics tab, an adenoma volume change tab, a cardiovascular/metabolic complication marker tab, a treatment scenario comparison tab, and a biomarker tab (SSTR occupancy · IGF-1 normalisation rate).
